# TSSA-7/B4 — Educational Content Authority and Evidence Boundaries

**Repository audit:** 25 October 2026 (IST) · **Status:** editorial correction + structural source audit in PR · **Production:** gated on CI, merge and live verification

## B4 contract and conservative interpretation

The B4 acceptance contract is **pedagogical review, independent references, appropriate author/expertise evidence and scoped editorial corrections**. We distinguish (1) source-format coverage, (2) a substantiated educational spot check, (3) scholarly relevance of cited evidence, (4) independently verified author qualifications, and (5) actual search/AI-engine recognition. One is **not proof** of the others.

Review scope:
- **168 core source owners**: 83 published editorial articles, 31 Phonics knowledge guides, 38 Grammar knowledge guides and 16 Vocabulary knowledge guides. These are B1/previous release source counts, not freshly verified provider indexed pages.
- **Nine prioritized editorial questions** covering Phonics start/decoding, SATPIN, Sight Words, reading fluency/comprehension, vocabulary, confidence and storytelling, with author-label/reference-section presence and source-file provenance.
- **One independently substantiated exact copy issue (A03-F003)** repaired at `/phonics-apps-for-preschoolers-india`: unsupported school-system common sound-order and outcome guarantee.

Output files generated by CI under `artifacts/tssa7-b4/`:
- `SOURCE_EDUCATIONAL_AUTHORITY_168.csv`: one row per source; subject family, whether an answer/educational example/practice exists, citation metadata, and exact file provenance where available. **Not a pedagogical quality grade**.
- `PRIORITY_9_BLOG_SPOT_CHECK.csv`: source-file and author-label/references-section inventory for nine priority articles; an **editorial spot check**, not an independent academic or clinical peer review.
- `SUMMARY.json`: counts, review exceptions and unverified-authority limitations. The CI fails when a governed source contract degrades or unsupported preschool claims reappear.

### Independently reviewed educational evidence

| Independent source | What it directly supports | What it does *not* establish |
|---|---|---|
| [Education Endowment Foundation — Phonics](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/phonics/) | Explicit and systematic phonics, taught correspondences, blending/segmenting, assessment matched to learners; broader language and comprehension needed | A universal CBSE/ICSE/IB sound order; approval of Tiny Steps or any preschool phonics app |
| [IES / What Works Clearinghouse — Foundational Skills](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/21/Published) | Segmental sound awareness and printed correspondences, decoding and word analysis, academic language and connected text practice; Kindergarten through Grade 3 context | Mandatory app session minutes, a guarantee of faster reading for multilingual children, or individual diagnosis |
| [NICHD — What is the best way to teach children to read?](https://www.nichd.nih.gov/health/topics/reading/conditioninfo/teach) | Phonemic awareness, phonics, fluency, vocabulary and comprehension as complementary evidence-based components | Phonics apps alone replacing oral language, responsive teaching or comprehension |

**B4 academic decision:** keep the teaching principle and practical example; remove unsupported institutional equivalence and universal promises. These sources are independent third-party guidance, **not validators, partners or endorsers** of Tiny Steps. English-system differences and accent variation require sensitivity: the relevant signal is clear modelling of the taught sound–spelling system and observed transfer, not an assumed official shared curriculum sequence.

### Exact editorial fix — `/phonics-apps-for-preschoolers-india`

Existing source contained both FAQ and selection checklist asserting `CBSE/ICSE/IB` share a school-aligned phonics sound order; the FAQ said such matching **"will be effective"** (A03-F003). This falsely conflated distinct school systems and an app's measured educational effect.

New page:
1. Defines alignment as **practice with already taught grapheme–phoneme correspondences**, not institutional sound order or endorsement.
2. Recasts ten minutes as an illustrative optional short parent-guided routine; no universal research-supported prescribed schedule.
3. Describes app support for bilingual/multilingual children **without guaranteed faster decoding**; advises evaluating transfer to fresh words.
4. Avoids promising b/d letter confusion will automatically resolve; where persistent, parents should seek qualified teacher/specialist discussion.
5. Provides **sat → pat** new-word transfer practice as an illustration, with explicit non-diagnostic caveat.
6. Adds visible EEF/IES evidence links, limitations and non-endorsement wording, with exact same canonical owner, H1, programme CTA, FAQ schema and site route.

**Out of scope:** No author biography, credential, independent educational-review signature, testimonial, school-board partnership, percentage mastery rate or implied scientific validation was invented. Existing source strings such as `author: Priya` and `author: Tiny Steps Research Desk` are **bylines only**, not third-party-verified academic expertise.
