## 1. Executive verdict

**COMPLETE WITH LIMITATIONS — not a full-platform certification.** Five intentional commercial URLs show publicly retrievable content: the broad [Grammar programme](https://tinystepslearning.com/grammar), separate [Writing programme](https://tinystepslearning.com/writing-classes-for-kids), [Beginner Grammar](https://tinystepslearning.com/courses/grammar), [Advanced Grammar](https://tinystepslearning.com/courses/grammar-mastery) and deliberately supportive [Beginner/Advanced/Writing chooser](https://tinystepslearning.com/english-grammar-writing-classes). Source manifest, route SEO registry and both relevant sitemap source files intend distinct self-canonical/indexable pages. Teaching copy sensibly distinguishes sentence mechanics (parts of speech, tense, agreement, punctuation, correction) from developing ideas, paragraphs, creative writing, self-editing and transferring skills to new writing tasks. Beginner and Advanced Grammar each publish 36 lessons, overlap ages 8–10 by design and explicitly use skill-led placement. A live web retriever followed the old /courses/advanced-grammar URL to /courses/grammar-mastery.

**Three specifically observed improvement areas:** **A05-F001 (P2)** the broad Grammar page's separately labelled “See Beginner Grammar curriculum” and “See Advanced Grammar curriculum” links both go to the **same generic /curriculum?tab=grammar page**, instead of their available differentiated level-specific course pages; **A05-F002 (P2)** the Writing landing provides good instructional examples but does **not publish a writing-specific intended age/readiness bracket or programme/level sequence**, unlike the Grammar side; **A05-F003 (P2)** the broad Grammar landing describes live correction and parent progress but offers **no worked original grammar-error correction / independent-transfer example or safe sample of how grammar progress is judged** on that page (Writing does have examples). These are on-page clarity/navigation gaps, not accusations that operational teaching is deficient.

The live/publicly retrieved Grammar text contains a branded reach claim and parent quotes, but testimonial permissions and 5000+/15+ claim substantiation were not independently reviewed. **Do not duplicate** the already-integrated A03-F002 and A01-F001 trust-evidence issue; cross-reference and validate with A16. The older combined grammar/writing URL intentionally remains an indexable *level chooser*, not a confirmed duplicate. Its actual search-query overlap is **A05-U001 (UNVERIFIED)** pending GSC query–page data.

**No P0/P1 live production defect was reproduced.** Raw HTTPS status, X-Robots-Tag, www redirects, initial HTML/DOM and deployed JSON-LD, Google-selected canonicals, actual index inclusion, native rankings, AI citations, lead conversion, accessibility and CWV remain unverified. Source SEO policy is not proof of deployed indexability; generic web-search findings are not proof of Google or Bing ranks or ChatGPT/Perplexity answers.
