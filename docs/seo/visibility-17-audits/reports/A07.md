## 1. Executive verdict (G0–G7)

**Gateway works as a navigable information architecture in accessible public web inspection.** The central page exposes seven correct, named destination hyperlinks (four /resources/ hubs plus /parents, /free-english-games-for-kids, /for-schools), an editorial-directory entry, and a three-layer question → concept → practice directory. This is *retrievability evidence in the inspection interface*, **NOT** independently measured wire-level 200, noindex absence, Search Console indexation, native Google/Bing ranks or AI citations.

**Confirmed defects/risks:** the gateway offers a speaking-game next step, but the linked speaking-games category itself states 0 playable now and the particular speaking practice game is Coming Soon (A07-F001). In checked-in source the gateway's SEO-description omits the Vocabulary pathway and the older shared subject list omits the newly linked Vocabulary hub (A07-F002/F004). A checked-in sitemap-static.xml is missing Vocabulary while the current route manifest and sitemap generator include it: **production sitemap membership is unresolved**, not proven broken (A07-F003 pending).

**Main recommendation:** preserve the successful seven-card layout, correct the speaking-game promise or route to presently playable alternatives, align descriptive/legacy registries, and test the generated, *deployed* sitemap plus GSC/Bing and native AI products before concluding the hierarchy is indexed or cited.
