## 1. Executive verdict and confidence boundary

**Verdict: COMPLETE WITH LIMITATIONS.** The public buyer journey is reasonably clear at text/source level: course-by-need routing → transparent **standard** pricing → one free 35-minute live 1:1 **assessment request** → later WhatsApp team slot discussion. The standard ₹400 per 35-minute 1:1 class with 12, 16 and 24-class amounts is consistent in public text and shared price configuration. Group prices and durations are distinguished. The site now correctly treats Summer Camp 2026 as an archive and Hyderabad as an **online** service area, not proof of a classroom there. Public contact page separates prospective admissions, existing parents and schools. These are real strengths from reader/source inspection; conversion completion has **not** been demonstrated. Source contracts additionally protect anonymous lead creation with Firestore shape validation, server-side attribution enrichment and dedupe, but deployment parity, existing production leads and admin delivery were not tested.

**New A16-specific findings (source-backed, NOT hypothetical P0/P1 live outages):** (1) draft assessment names/number persist in browser `localStorage` while typing, with no point-of-entry adjacent retention notice visible in the shared form source (A16-F001); (2) public client validates phone as merely non-empty, whereas anonymous Firestore create requires a structurally minimum-length phone, making some user inputs guaranteed to fail under the checked-in rules (A16-F002); (3) analytics emit a “demo booking complete” event for a successfully saved *request*, before an appointment slot is confirmed (A16-F003); (4) Ultra Premium source configuration's exact per-class and 12-class amounts disagree by **₹11 in each of six formats**, and a separate 4× native-tier constant defines ₹1,600, rather than Ultra 1:1 ₹1,899 (A16-F004; do not infer actual invoice error); (5) 35 all-five-star curated static parent excerpts have no per-item signed-consent provenance in the inspected source data, though a safe consent process is documented separately (A16-F005; no quote declared false or unauthorised); and (6) /courses’ first-step chooser lacks a distinct conversational Spoken English route even though `/pricing` and `/book-demo` distinguish it from public speaking (A16-F006, parent routing improvement). Source and sampled public-reader limits are explicit per finding.

**NOT reproduced:** failed real form submission, loss of a valid lead, duplicate/malformed payment, unauthorized data leak, privacy-law breach, false parent quote, actual unusable video gallery, sold expired summer camp, Google/Bing deindexing, AI source citations. These belong in pending verification until genuine evidence exists. A16 did NOT call clients, read Firestore content, view analytics dashboards, change production rules or send a test message.
