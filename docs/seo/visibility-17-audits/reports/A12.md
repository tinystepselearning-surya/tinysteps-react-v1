## 1. Executive assessment (G0–G7)

**Evidence-supported outcome:** The Parents Hub is a functioning information-discovery surface in the public web reader: concern-first reading, grammar, writing and speaking cards; clear assessment-led parent decision explanations; 10 child-guide routes; a research-guide handoff; pricing/course/demo navigation; observable progress advice; and multilingual, low-pressure home-practice suggestions. Every published parent-hub URL listed in `public/sitemap-parents.xml` is retrievable as meaningful external reader text, while the current manifest assigns index/self-canonical intent. This is **not proof** of response-code 200, server-rendered first HTML, Google/Bing indexing, actual citation by an AI engine, or a working demo lead submission.

**Main A12 findings:** diagnosis-like wording on concern routing and course-choice copy (A12-F001); missing direct back-to-Parents Hub links on 9 of 10 child pages despite source breadcrumb JSON-LD on most (A12-F002); claims of “research-backed” / “science-backed” without clearly attributable parent-page primary research evidence in the observed extract (A12-F003); a 7-day beginner phonics example that can outrun the sound set used to illustrate the preceding steps (A12-F004); and operational billing/scheduling promise transparency that is worth strengthening but **does not establish the policies are false** (A12-F005).

**Critical safety overlap:** The connected `/shy-child-speaking-confidence` page generalizes a quiet child as “usually not just personality” and includes eye-contact avoidance among signs; this issue **already has canonical ID [A06-F002](./A06.md)**, with A12 as content remediation owner. **Do not invent A12-F006 for the same problem.** Unlike that older support landing, `/parents/speech-confidence` explicitly advises discussion with the school and appropriate qualified professional if silence persists or causes significant distress; this is an important safer pattern to reuse, not a claim the child has a medical condition.

**P0/P1:** No reproducible P0 or live P1 technical/index failure established. Parent operational rules, first-response/meta and platform visibility remain validation gates; never promote tool errors or source intent to an actual search-engine result.
