## 1. Executive verdict (evidence vs unknown)

**Verdict — COMPLETE WITH LIMITATIONS, NOT a verified indexing/ranking/AI-citation pass.** Both canonical Reading commercial pages were retrievable as web pages on 17 October 2026. They communicate an academically sensible distinction: /reading-classes-for-kids targets connected-text accuracy, vocabulary, comprehension, retelling, phrasing and general reading support; /reading-fluency-program targets fluent, accurate, expressive passage reading when decoding is reasonably stable. Both explain when phonics is the better initial pathway, avoid a simplistic speed-only goal, link to 35-minute teacher-led free assessment and link to parent-problem help. Source registry/manifest and sitemap list both self-canonical and index-intended.

**Main substantiated improvements:** A04-F001 (P2): parent-facing commercial reading pages describe assessment/progress but provide no illustrative worked **reading-specific baseline-to-progress sample or observable scoring rubric** on those pages, despite existing teaching narratives; this is an evidence/detail gap rather than proof instruction does not measure progress. A04-F002 (P2): a child-problem card for “reads the words but cannot explain the story” on /reading-classes-for-kids sends readers to a generic decoding-to-comprehension bridge even though the repository explicitly assigns that diagnostic search intent to the more fitting dedicated story-comprehension guide; another section already links the right guide. A04-F003 (P3): /reading-fluency-program does not link directly to the existing evidence-rich fluency article despite relevant source/ownership records (link opportunity, not broken page).

**Unverified:** exact deployed HTTP status and redirects, robots and X-Robots, initial HTML prerender/meta/JSON-LD, Google-selected canonicals, first-party indexing, query impressions/clicks, Google/Bing placement, bot server logs, actual ChatGPT Search/Perplexity/Copilot/Google AI citations, physical-device accessibility, forms, parental consent and real lead attribution. **No P0/P1 production defect reproduced.** Neither /reading nor a presumed /reading-comprehension-classes-for-kids URL is accepted as canonical without route evidence.
