## 1. Executive summary and verdict

**Observed:** The [school hub](https://tinystepslearning.com/for-schools) presents a clearly differentiated B2B proposition, with the school employing its own teachers while Tiny Steps supplies systematic phonics scope/sequence, classroom content, progressive training/rehearsal, diagnostic guidance, a learning partner and up to a year of support. The live web-text representation displays three annual tiers (₹59,000 + GST, ₹1.49 lakh + GST, ₹2.99 lakh + GST) and an eight-week pilot (₹24,900 + GST), with teacher/learner/campus limits and upgrade credit conditions. It explicitly separates **CBSE/NCF curriculum expectations** from a **private provider's implementation programme** and disclaims CBSE, NCERT and UK-DfE endorsements. Official NCF and DfE publications substantiate the *general educational principles*, **not** the specific efficacy, completion, accreditation or existence of Tiny Steps' school programme.

**Eight relevant school/research articles** are readable at their independent `/blog/...` canonical-intent routes and appear in source audience and checked-in sitemap indexes. Only four appear in the hub's prominent evidence-library cards; direct featured routes for teacher-training, assessment, systematic implementation and the “letter sounds alone” educational problem would make the school pathway more useful. Original, school-specific sample lesson/trainer credentials/demonstrable partner implementation records and measurable teacher/school outcomes were not externally evidenced in the material reviewed. Institutional procurement details beyond plan price/limits remain largely to be requested by WhatsApp.

**Most important risks:** These are predominantly **P2 content, navigation and institutional trust improvements** (A14-F001–F006), not proof of false claims or broken service. Source/live reader difference in the displayed intended age (A14-H001) needs actual rendered/live-head verification before correction. **No live P0/P1 index or availability failure was reproduced.**

**No Google/Bing rankings, index counts, AI recommendations, partnership leads, form completion, classroom training outcomes or named school results are independently verified.** The report's score is **NOT SCORABLE** rather than artificially zero.
