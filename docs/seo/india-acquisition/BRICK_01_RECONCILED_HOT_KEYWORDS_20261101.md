## High-volume terms excluded from the core online children market

- `phonics classes near me`: KWE **18,100** India average/month — offline/local location intent.
- `public speaking classes near me`: KWE **6,600** — audience likely includes adults and physical centres.
- `phonics classes`: KWE **6,600** — generic mixed online/offline, not the same as child-online demand.
- `public speaking classes online`: KWE **1,000** — online but adult and child intentions mixed; secondary research only.
- `grammar classes`: KWE **720** — adult/child and offline/online mixed.
- `english tuition classes near me`: KWE **480** — local tuition.
- Semrush Keyword Magic seed `grammar classes` displayed **13,867 keyword suggestions / aggregate volume 978,260**: dominated by grammar worksheets, school tests, Hindi grammar and textbook answers. **Absolutely not** a market-size estimate for paid online Grammar classes for children.
