## Audit rules

- **strong**: the article already contains specific diagnostic, instructional, assessment, or implementation logic that is useful on its own.
- **partial**: useful practical guidance exists, but the Tiny Steps contribution is generic, implicit, or not clearly separated from general advice.
- **none**: the article is primarily general guidance/research and does not currently contain a distinct first-party contribution.
- **enrich now** only when the repository already supports the addition.
- **founder confirmation required** when a stronger first-party claim would be useful but the exact Tiny Steps operational practice is not safely established in the repository.
- First-party practice is not labelled as research unless an external source independently supports it.
