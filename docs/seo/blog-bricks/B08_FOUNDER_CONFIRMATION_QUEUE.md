These questions are intentionally left unanswered. They would strengthen future first-party sections, but the current repository does not establish the exact Tiny Steps operational practice strongly enough to publish them as fact.

## `spoken-english-classes-for-kids-confidence`

**Question:** Does the Tiny Steps speaking programme use a standard progression for independent responses, such as sentence frame → complete sentence → reason/example → unprompted response, across regular classes?

**Why it matters:** The article would benefit from a concrete Tiny Steps speaking checkpoint rather than generic confidence language.

**Example of the type of answer needed:** The actual sequence teachers are expected to use, what counts as readiness to reduce prompts, and what is observed before moving to the next speaking task.

## `online-phonics-classes-vs-school`

**Question:** What exact diagnostic or feedback step does a Tiny Steps 1:1 phonics class consistently add that a parent may not receive in a normal whole-class school lesson?

**Why it matters:** A first-party comparison is useful only if it describes a real Tiny Steps operating practice rather than making a generic online-vs-school sales claim.

**Example of the type of answer needed:** A concrete lesson or assessment loop such as what the teacher checks, how the child retries, and how the next activity changes from that evidence.

## `why-parents-choose-online-phonics`

**Question:** Which reasons for choosing Tiny Steps online phonics are supported by actual parent feedback or operational records, rather than assumptions?

**Why it matters:** Publishing “parents choose us because…” without a reliable basis would turn B8 into unsupported marketing.

**Example of the type of answer needed:** Verifiable recurring reasons from documented enquiries, feedback, or programme records that can be stated without inventing percentages, testimonials, or outcomes.
