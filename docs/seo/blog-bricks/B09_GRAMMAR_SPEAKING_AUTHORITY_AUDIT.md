The audit uses the current 76-record blog registry as the universe. Twenty-four records are directly relevant or adjacent to Grammar/Speaking authority; the remaining 52 are outside B9's topical scope and should retain their current ownership unless a later cross-link is genuinely useful.

- B8 first-party diagnostic ladders in the four parent diagnostic pages above must remain intact.

## Executive findings

### Grammar

The Grammar cluster already has a workable authority structure but it is not linked as one.

- Broad indexable owner: `week-7-grammar-nouns-to-paragraphs`
- Diagnostic owner — sentence formation: `how-to-improve-sentence-formation-in-kids`
- Diagnostic owner — rule/application transfer: `child-knows-grammar-but-makes-mistakes`
- Noindex skill satellites: tenses, conjunctions, subject-verb agreement, creative writing, assessment, editing, and oral-to-written transfer.

The main weakness is **authority flow**, not missing content. The strongest parent diagnostic pages currently sit in the `Parent Guides` discovery lane because their source category is `Parent Tips`, while the `Grammar` lane is dominated by Week-series content. Contextual links between the broad owner, the diagnostics, and the skill satellites are sparse.

### Speaking & Communication

The Speaking cluster also already has a broad indexable owner:

- Broad indexable owner: `week-12-speaking-confidence-seeds`
- Diagnostic owner — understands but does not speak: `child-understands-english-but-does-not-speak`
- Diagnostic owner — one-word answers: `child-gives-one-word-answers`
- Noindex skill satellites: structure, visual aids, debate, video feedback, competition preparation, and family showcase.

Again, the main weakness is **authority flow and discovery placement**, not lack of content. The two strongest diagnosis pages sit in the `Parent Guides` lane rather than `Speaking & Communication`.

### New URL decision from Phase 1

**Current recommendation: 0 new B9 blog URLs.**

Reasons:

1. Broad Grammar coverage already exists on the indexable `week-7-grammar-nouns-to-paragraphs` page.
2. Broad Speaking confidence/communication coverage already exists on the indexable `week-12-speaking-confidence-seeds` page.
3. The strongest parent problems already have protected diagnostic owners.
4. Creating new broad pillars would introduce avoidable overlap and migration work when the real gap is internal authority architecture.

## Structural discovery finding

Current discovery metadata is category-driven:

- source `Grammar` → discovery `Grammar`
- source `Public Speaking` or `English Communication` → discovery `Speaking & Communication`
- source `Parent Tips` → discovery `Parent Guides`

This means four of the most important B9 parent authority pages are currently hidden from their topical lanes:

### Grammar pages currently shown as Parent Guides

- `how-to-improve-sentence-formation-in-kids`
- `child-knows-grammar-but-makes-mistakes`

### Speaking pages currently shown as Parent Guides

- `child-understands-english-but-does-not-speak`
- `child-gives-one-word-answers`

**B9 recommendation:** keep their source category and URLs unchanged, but add explicit discovery-category overrides so these four pages surface in the topic lanes they actually own. This is a discovery read-model refinement, not a source-category rewrite.
