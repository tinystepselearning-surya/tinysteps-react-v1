# R21 — High-Value Speaking & Communication Content

Revision: `2026-09-10-r21`

## Scope

R21 executes only the three genuine CREATE gaps identified by the audit-first R20 Speaking & Communication Knowledge Architecture. It does not regenerate the ten strong R20 KEEP pages and does not act on the held legacy consolidation candidate.

Published parent guides:

1. `/blog/conversation-skills-for-kids`
2. `/blog/how-to-teach-storytelling-to-kids`
3. `/blog/public-speaking-delivery-for-kids`

No other R20 content proposal is executed.

## Why these three pages exist

### Conversation skills

Tiny Steps already owned response expansion, confidence and debate resources, but no page owned ordinary reciprocal conversation. The new guide combines listening, turn-taking, staying on topic, relevant response expansion, follow-up questions and communication repair in one substantial parent utility rather than creating thin pages for each subskill.

### Oral storytelling and retelling

The existing Story Cards guide remains a useful activity owner and the speech-structure guide remains the planned-presentation owner. Neither fully owns the parent intent of teaching a child to retell or create an oral narrative. The R21 guide separates retelling from story creation and focuses on meaningful sequence, relevant detail, listener clarity, oral rehearsal and decreasing prompt dependence.

### Public-speaking delivery

Pace, volume, pauses, emphasis and audience awareness previously appeared across confidence, video-feedback and competition resources without one clear informational owner. R21 creates that owner while explicitly defining delivery around intelligibility and meaning—not accent conformity, forced eye contact or theatrical performance.

## R20 / R21 governance boundary

R20 remains the historical planning layer:

- its three CREATE records remain `proposal-only`, with no path or canonical topic ID on the R20 records themselves;
- R21 adds a separate execution registry and publishes exactly those three proposed paths;
- the R20 audit supports an explicit `--r21-executed` downstream mode while preserving its original pre-R21 behavior when the flag is absent.

This follows the same planning-versus-execution separation used by R14→R15 and R17→R18.

## Canonical ownership

R21 adds exactly three additive informational owners on top of R19:

- `conversation-skills-guide` → `/blog/conversation-skills-for-kids`
- `oral-storytelling-retelling-guide` → `/blog/how-to-teach-storytelling-to-kids`
- `public-speaking-delivery-guide` → `/blog/public-speaking-delivery-for-kids`

All three use `/resources/speaking` as the subject hub.

The existing commercial/informational ownership remains unchanged:

- `/resources/speaking` — informational subject discovery
- `/speaking` — live public-speaking programme
- `/spoken-english-classes-for-kids-online` — spoken-English commercial route

## Evidence and inclusion boundaries

The three guides use authoritative curriculum/evidence sources such as GOV.UK English programmes of study, the Australian Curriculum and the Education Endowment Foundation oral-language evidence summary.

Important limits are stated in the content:

- oral-language evidence does not prove Tiny Steps' named editorial routines;
- EEF's oral-language evidence does not primarily evaluate formal public-speaking delivery;
- communication quality is not accent conformity;
- confidence is not loudness or extroversion;
- constant eye contact is not required;
- multilingual variation by listener, setting, topic or language is not itself a deficit;
- Tiny Steps educational content does not diagnose speech, language, hearing, anxiety or developmental conditions.

## Discovery strategy

R21 deliberately avoids enlarging the already-structured Resources hub with another large content section. The new pages enter the normal blog discovery and sitemap system immediately. R22 will provide the stronger bidirectional semantic-journey layer from established speaking resources into these new owners, matching the architecture already used for Reading and Grammar/Writing.

This keeps the Resources UX compact while separating content publication from semantic graph execution.

## Legacy consolidation hold

`/blog/spoken-english-classes-for-kids-confidence` remains a consolidation candidate only. R21 performs no deletion, redirect, canonical transfer or URL change. Any future consolidation requires fresh URL/search/link evidence.

## Non-goals

R21 does not:

- publish thin pages for individual conversation or delivery subskills;
- rewrite the ten R20 KEEP resources for artificial freshness;
- change commercial programme routes;
- create a new speaking taxonomy;
- create runtime semantic journeys (R22 owns that work);
- claim GSC, GA4, reviewer or teacher evidence that is not present;
- merge itself automatically.

## Acceptance

R21 is ready only when:

1. exactly three R20 CREATE decisions are explicitly executed;
2. all three pages are substantial, parent-facing and indexable;
3. the three additive canonical owners are unique and collision-free;
4. R20 planning records remain unchanged and pass `--r21-executed` downstream validation;
5. the ten R20 KEEP resources remain present;
6. the legacy consolidation candidate remains on HOLD;
7. `/resources/speaking`, `/speaking` and `/spoken-english-classes-for-kids-online` retain their roles;
8. all relevant R0–R21 tests/audits, typecheck, production build/prerender, rendered checks and SEO smoke pass.
