# R20 — Speaking & Communication Knowledge Architecture

Revision: `2026-09-09-r20`

## Scope

R20 is audit-first architecture only. It maps the existing speaking and communication library, protects strong owners and records a small number of future editorial gaps. It publishes no article, creates no route, transfers no canonical owner and changes no redirect.

The nine domains are response initiation, response expansion, confidence/context transfer, conversation/listening, storytelling/retelling, speech organisation, delivery/audience connection, discussion/reasoning and rehearsal/feedback/transfer. They are connected dimensions, not a rigid developmental ladder.

## Ownership boundary

- `/resources/speaking` remains the informational discovery hub.
- `/speaking` remains the commercial public-speaking programme owner.
- `/spoken-english-classes-for-kids-online` remains the commercial spoken-English owner.
- Existing substantial articles remain their established informational, diagnostic or practice owners.
- CREATE records have no `path`, canonical topic ID or publication approval.

## Content audit

- **KEEP — 10:** established confidence, diagnostic, organisation, visual-aid, debate, feedback, competition, home-practice and storytelling-bridge resources.
- **CONSOLIDATE CANDIDATE / HOLD — 1:** `/blog/spoken-english-classes-for-kids-confidence` overlaps the stronger confidence roadmap. R20 authorizes no deletion, redirect or canonical change. The repository already contains historical routing/indexing treatment from earlier SEO work; that pre-existing state is outside this brick and is not expanded here. Any further change requires fresh URL, link and GSC evidence.
- **CREATE PROPOSAL — 3:** conversation skills, oral storytelling/retelling and public-speaking delivery. These records are planning inputs only.

Proposed paths, deliberately unpublished:

- `/blog/conversation-skills-for-kids`
- `/blog/how-to-teach-storytelling-to-kids`
- `/blog/public-speaking-delivery-for-kids`

## Educational and inclusion boundaries

- Audible, intelligible communication is not accent conformity.
- Confidence is not loudness or extroversion.
- Constant eye contact is not required.
- Multilingual variation by listener, setting, topic or language is not itself a deficit.
- R20 makes no medical, developmental or diagnostic claim.
- Feedback targets one observable communication behavior at a time and allows optional recording and retry.

## Regression boundary

R20 adds no runtime semantic-journey adapter. Tests and audits pin representative Brick 6, R16 and R19 outputs so this planning layer cannot silently alter existing educational pathways.

## Acceptance

R20 is complete only when:

1. all nine domains are unique, connected and non-rigid;
2. all KEEP sources still exist;
3. registered KEEP owners resolve to their existing canonical paths;
4. the three CREATE records remain absent from blog publication, route, SEO and rendered output;
5. the legacy candidate remains HOLD with no R20 URL-change authorization;
6. commercial and informational speaking owners remain unchanged;
7. Brick 6, R16 and R19 representative semantic outputs remain exact;
8. typecheck, build, prerender, rendered audits, sitemap/canonical/indexability, SEO smoke and shadowing checks pass.
