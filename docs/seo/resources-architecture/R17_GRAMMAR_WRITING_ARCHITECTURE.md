# Resources R17 — Grammar & Writing Knowledge Architecture

Revision: `2026-09-09-r17`

## Purpose

R17 applies the same audit-first discipline used for reading to the Grammar & Writing resource pathway. It does not publish new content. It maps the knowledge system, protects strong existing intent owners, and records only genuine gaps worth executing later.

## Knowledge domains

The architecture uses nine interacting domains:

1. Word classes & morphology
2. Sentence core
3. Sentence expansion
4. Verb systems: tense & agreement
5. Clauses & connectives
6. Punctuation & conventions
7. Cohesion & paragraphs
8. Composition & idea development
9. Editing & independent transfer

This is **not** a claim that children learn grammar and writing in one fixed staircase. Children may work across multiple domains at once. Oral language, vocabulary, reading, grammar and writing interact, and grammar labels are useful only when they support clearer meaning and independent use.

R17 keeps these established resources rather than generating replacements:

The broad roadmap already introduces nouns, verbs, adjectives and pronouns in context. R17 therefore rejects a generic duplicate “parts of speech” pillar unless later evidence shows a materially different parent need.

## Controlled gaps

Only two CREATE gaps are recorded.

### 1. Punctuation & capital letters for kids

Proposed path: `/blog/punctuation-and-capital-letters-for-kids`

Why it is useful:
- punctuation appears throughout existing material but does not have one clear parent-facing owner;
- parents need a usable explanation of sentence boundaries, capitals and beginner punctuation choices;
- one substantial conventions guide is better than separate thin pages for full stops, question marks, exclamation marks and capitals.

### 2. Paragraph writing for kids

Proposed path: `/blog/how-to-teach-paragraph-writing-to-kids`

Why it is useful:
- the broad roadmap reaches paragraphs, but does not own the full paragraph-teaching intent;
- creative-writing material addresses idea scaffolds rather than the specific transition from connected sentences to one coherent paragraph;
- a future guide should cover main idea, relevant detail, order, cohesion and revision without presenting one rigid paragraph formula as universal.

## Non-goals

R17 does not:

- create the two planned articles;
- split every grammar term into a separate SEO page;
- change `/resources/grammar` UX;
- alter `/grammar` or `/writing-classes-for-kids` commercial ownership;
- rewrite existing tenses, conjunction, subject–verb, editing or assessment guides;
- claim a universal age-by-age grammar sequence;
- add fake modified dates to existing articles.
