# Post-R13 Reading Knowledge Follow-up

R13 is intentionally limited to the phonics sound/word utility. It does not expand the editorial reading library in the same change.

The next reading-knowledge architecture should treat these as connected but distinct knowledge families:

1. Phonemic awareness
2. Phonics and decoding
3. Reading fluency
4. Vocabulary
5. Reading comprehension
6. Spelling and encoding

For future research/guides, Tiny Steps may study the information architecture and educational depth of established reading-intervention/reference sites such as Read Naturally, while writing original Tiny Steps material and checking scientific claims against appropriate primary or authoritative research sources.

This follow-up must preserve existing canonical owners. In particular, existing fluency, parent-problem, programme, blog-pillar, practice, and commercial routes should be audited before creating any new reading-knowledge URL.
