# KB-FINAL — Cross-Knowledge-Base Integration & Freeze

Revision: `2026-09-10-kb-final`

## Mission

Close the Tiny Steps informational knowledge-base programme after the independently frozen Phonics & Reading, Grammar & Writing, and Speaking & Communication sessions have landed on `main`.

KB-FINAL is an integration and governance brick only. It does not add articles, reopen PH/GR/SP expansion, create keyword landing pages, or begin the Commercial SEO & Lead Growth programme.

## Frozen inputs

- **Session A — Phonics & Reading:** PH0–PH7 frozen and merged.
- **Session B — Grammar & Writing:** GR1–GR7 frozen and merged.
- **Session C — Speaking & Communication:** SP0–SP6 frozen and merged.

The dedicated closure tests remain the evidence sources for each session:

- `src/tests/seo/sessionAPhonicsReadingClosure.spec.ts`
- `src/tests/seo/grammarWritingGr7Closure.spec.ts`
- `src/tests/seo/resourcesSP6SpeakingCommunicationCompletion.spec.ts`

## Protected knowledge hubs

The three Resources subject hubs remain informational discovery/practice orchestrators only:

- `/resources/phonics`
- `/resources/grammar`
- `/resources/speaking`

They must not absorb programme, price, comparison or conversion ownership.

## Protected commercial/conversion handoff routes

KB-FINAL protects, without commercially optimising, these downstream routes:

- `/phonics`
- `/reading-classes-for-kids`
- `/grammar`
- `/writing-classes-for-kids`
- `/speaking`
- `/spoken-english-classes-for-kids-online`
- `/online-english-classes-for-kids`
- `/book-demo`

Where an intent is already represented in the historical canonical topic registry, the exact topic-to-owner relationship is frozen. Newer standalone commercial routes are protected through the public route manifest rather than being forced into an older ownership registry during this closure brick.

## Integration gates

KB-FINAL passes only when all of the following are true:

1. Sessions A, B and C still report their agreed frozen states.
2. All protected knowledge hubs are indexable, prerendered, sitemap-eligible and self-canonical.
3. Knowledge hubs own informational subject discovery only.
4. Protected commercial/conversion routes remain distinct from knowledge hubs and retain valid public-route contracts.
5. Existing canonical topic IDs and query-intent ownership remain globally unique.
6. Frozen parent-problem layers retain valid existing owner/practice handoffs; held Phonics problems do not silently acquire new URLs.
7. Phonics/Reading and Grammar/Writing semantic graphs remain closed without orphan regression, while Speaking Tier-1 owners remain routed to established public content.
8. Informational expansion remains frozen and Commercial SEO/keyword research remain explicitly downstream work.
9. The three dedicated session closure tests plus the KB-FINAL test pass together.
10. Full repository tests, historical Resources audits, canonical/shadowing checks, TypeScript, production build/prerender, rendered audits and SEO smoke pass on the exact branch head.

## Publication policy

After KB-FINAL:

- informational expansion is **frozen**;
- thin micro-pages and duplicate-intent pages remain disallowed;
- PH/GR/SP may reopen only for a demonstrated user, curriculum or search-intent gap;
- commercial keyword research, commercial page optimisation, CRO, buyer/fee/comparison expansion and lead-growth work belong to the separate Commercial SEO & Lead Growth project.

## Downstream handoff

The next programme starts at **C0 — Revenue Measurement, Lead Definition & Data Integrity**, followed by **C1 — Commercial Demand Intelligence** and **C2 — Canonical Commercial Ownership & Cannibalisation Audit**.

KB-FINAL deliberately does not pre-optimise commercial pages or pre-decide new commercial URLs. Those decisions require C0/C1 evidence.

## Frozen end state

> **TINY STEPS KNOWLEDGE BASE = FROZEN**

The architecture may later change because evidence changes priorities. New ideas alone do not reopen this knowledge-base programme.
