# R18 — High-Value Grammar & Writing Content Execution

## Goal

Execute only the two genuine Grammar & Writing content gaps identified by R17, while preserving the strong existing grammar ecosystem and the established commercial owners.

R18 is an execution brick, not a broad content-generation wave.

## Published gaps

### 1. Punctuation and capital letters for kids

Canonical path:

`/blog/punctuation-and-capital-letters-for-kids`

Intent:

`punctuation and capital letters for kids`

Why it exists:

The existing grammar roadmap and editing resources mention punctuation, but no single parent-facing page owns the practical beginner problem of sentence boundaries, capitals and end punctuation.

Teaching boundary:

- sentence meaning before mark labels;
- capital at sentence beginnings and for appropriate proper names;
- full stops, question marks and exclamation marks chosen from sentence purpose;
- commas introduced through a real grammatical function, not the incomplete “take a breath” rule;
- editing and independent transfer treated as different demands;
- no thin page network for individual punctuation marks.

### 2. Paragraph writing for kids

Canonical path:

`/blog/how-to-teach-paragraph-writing-to-kids`

Intent:

`how to teach paragraph writing to kids`

Why it exists:

Tiny Steps already has strong sentence-formation, grammar-roadmap and creative-writing material, but no dedicated owner for the transition from secure sentences to one coherent paragraph.

Teaching boundary:

- one main idea;
- relevant supporting detail;
- logical order;
- cohesion across sentences;
- oral rehearsal;
- rereading and revision;
- no universal five-sentence paragraph rule.

## Evidence boundary

Both pages use authoritative educational/curriculum sources for broad principles:

- IES / What Works Clearinghouse — *Teaching Elementary School Students to Be Effective Writers*;
- GOV.UK — *National curriculum in England: English programmes of study*.

Tiny Steps routines are presented as editorial teaching guidance, not as standardized interventions or universal age prescriptions.
