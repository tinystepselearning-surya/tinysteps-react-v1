## Competitor/provider patterns observed

Current commercial SERPs repeatedly expose these decision attributes:

- live teacher-led teaching
- 1:1 versus small group
- age range
- session duration
- curriculum/syllabus visibility
- free or low-cost demo
- fees or starting price
- progress reporting
- confidence / fluency outcomes
- blending / reading transfer outcomes for phonics
- storytelling, debate, presentation and communication outcomes for speaking

Examples observed in current results include PlanetSpark, BrightCHAMPS, JuniorSpark, WizMantra, Klariti Learning, Victory Fluent Forum, UrbanPro and The Learning Barn Academy.

## C1 interpretation

The commercial opportunity is not simply to add more pages. The research shows substantial overlap among provider, solution, comparison, fee and trial intents. C2 must decide whether each cluster belongs to an existing commercial owner, should be consolidated, or is distinct enough to justify a new owner.

## Data-quality rule

Search-result observation proves that an intent exists in the current market but does not prove Tiny Steps query volume, rank, CTR or conversion rate. Those numeric fields remain null until authenticated query-level evidence is available.
