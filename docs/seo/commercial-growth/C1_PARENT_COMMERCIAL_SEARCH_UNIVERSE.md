# C1 — Parent Commercial Search Universe

Revision: 2026-09-10-c1-r3  
Status: research-complete  
Depends on: C0 frozen, KB-FINAL frozen

## Mission

Build one classified commercial-search universe for parents considering Tiny Steps across phonics, reading, grammar, writing, spoken English, public speaking, communication, broad online English and 1:1 tutoring.

C1 is research only. It does not change public copy, titles, H1s, URLs, canonicals or keyword ownership. C2 owns canonical commercial keyword ownership.

## Parent decision journey

Every researched query is classified against the parent journey:

Problem → Solution → Provider research → Comparison → Price → Trial/demo → Enrolment

## Required accounting fields

The machine-readable registry at `src/lib/commercialC1SearchUniverse.ts` records query, subject, intent, parent stage, demand signal, commercial strength, existing observed Tiny Steps surface, conversion relevance, recommended research action, evidence and notes. Observed first-party Google/Bing metrics are stored separately in `src/lib/commercialC1ObservedSearchEvidence.ts` so modeled research rows are not falsely presented as measured query telemetry.

Allowed research actions are KEEP, OPTIMISE, CONSOLIDATE, BUILD and HOLD. These are research recommendations, not implementation authorization.

## Observed Google evidence

The three-month GSC export covers 9 June–8 September 2026 and records 6,612 clicks, 107,515 impressions, 6.15% calculated CTR and approximately 7.55 impression-weighted position across the property.

The commercial-query family evidence shows phonics is currently the strongest mature commercial search family. The normalized evidence layer records 103 classified commercial phonics rows with 7,604 impressions and 124 clicks inside the top-1,000 query extract.

International whole-site visibility is already observed in the same export, including USA, UK, Australia, UAE and Singapore. This proves geographic discovery but does not by itself justify separate country landing pages.

## Observed Bing evidence

Bing independently shows commercial phonics and 1:1 English visibility, including online phonics classes, phonics fee/cost research and 1:1 English-class wording.

Bing AI also cites Tiny Steps for commercial phonics queries such as `phonics classes for kids` and `online phonics classes`, with `/phonics` itself receiving recorded AI page citations. Therefore AI commercial visibility for phonics is observed, not merely modeled through synthetic prompts.

The Bing export files do not encode their selected report period, so C1 does not time-normalize Bing totals against the 92-day Google report.

## International + AI research

The international matrix covers 11 commercial families across six audiences: broad English, phonics, reading, grammar, creative writing, spoken English, public speaking, communication, 1:1 tutor, fees and free trial/demo.

The AI-style layer models natural parent questions across the complete decision journey, including problem diagnosis, provider selection, price/value, 1:1-versus-group comparison, timezone/curriculum fit, trial/demo and post-demo enrolment decisions.

These are research prompts, not proposed SEO pages.

## Operating priors

CRM enrolment tracking is not yet systematic. C1 therefore records the owner's operational knowledge only as declared planning priors:

- approximately 1 enrolment per 3 completed demos (~33.3%);
- approximately 1 parent per 5 leads drops because ₹400/class is outside budget (~20%).

These must not be multiplied into a fabricated observed lead-to-enrolment rate.

## Strategic signal

Tiny Steps does not have a general shortage of informational authority. Phonics already has meaningful generic, comparison, pricing and AI visibility; other commercial programmes are less mature. The next task is to concentrate each commercial intent on one evidence-backed owner rather than create more overlapping pages.

That ownership decision is C2.
