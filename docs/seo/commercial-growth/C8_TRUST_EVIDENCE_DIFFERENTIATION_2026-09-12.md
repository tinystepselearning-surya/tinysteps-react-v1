## Evidence-backed differentiation pillars

1. **Observable before purchase** — parents can inspect class samples rather than relying only on marketing claims.
2. **Responsive teacher-led delivery** — modelling, prompting, correction and support respond to learner performance.
3. **Structured skill progression** — the curriculum is staged but not forced into one rigid age-only sequence.
4. **Proof with boundaries** — first-party parent feedback is individual experience, not a guarantee of the same result for every child.
5. **Named academic ownership** — organization, founder and academic-design authority are connected through dedicated entity surfaces.

## Claim-safety contract

C8 does not permit:

- unsupported credentials
- unsupported affiliations or endorsement claims
- guaranteed outcomes
- fabricated review claims
- treating third-party citations as endorsements
- treating individual parent feedback as universal learner outcomes
