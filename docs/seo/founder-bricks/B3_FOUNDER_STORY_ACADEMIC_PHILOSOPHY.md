# Brick 3 — Founder story and academic philosophy

## Purpose

Expand the canonical founder page with evidence-backed context about why Tiny Steps Learning was founded, what Priya leads academically, and the teaching principles already documented elsewhere on the Tiny Steps site.

## Added in this brick

- Founder story section using the existing approved statement that Tiny Steps was founded to make English learning more structured, personal and measurable for children.
- Academic leadership section covering the existing responsibility areas: Curriculum Direction, Teacher Development and Academic Quality.
- Academic philosophy section grounded in existing Tiny Steps language around readiness, prerequisites, guided practice, retrieval, cumulative review, gradual reduction of support and observable learner progress.
- A dedicated content module so founder-page claims remain centralized and auditable.
- Focused page tests for the story, leadership scope and academic principles.

## Evidence basis

The brick reuses or conservatively synthesizes claims already present on the current Team page and its supporting SEO/trust documentation:

- Priya founded Tiny Steps Learning to make high-quality English learning more structured, personal and measurable for children.
- Her academic role covers curriculum development, lesson design, teacher guidance, teaching quality and parent communication across Phonics, Reading, Grammar, Writing and Public Speaking.
- Progress is observed through reading accuracy, sentence formation, language use and increasingly independent communication.
- Learning progression is informed by readiness/prerequisites, guided practice, retrieval, cumulative review and gradual reduction of support.

## Guardrails

This brick deliberately does not add:

- degrees, certifications or qualifications;
- years-of-experience claims;
- awards or rankings;
- previous-employer history;
- founder quotes presented as verbatim quotations;
- Person/ProfilePage schema changes;
- `sameAs` or LinkedIn links;
- sitemap or broad internal-link authority changes.

Those remain separate later bricks.
