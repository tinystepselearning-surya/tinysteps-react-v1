# Phonics landing-page narrative polish

## Objective

Improve `/phonics` as a premium, parent-first landing journey without weakening or rewriting the protected Phonics Brick 1 SEO/AEO/GEO authority contract.

The optimisation is consolidation and presentation work, not a new SEO expansion.

## Protected baseline

Do not disturb:

- `/phonics` as the main programme/authority owner
- `/best-online-phonics-classes-for-kids-in-india` as the buyer/comparison owner
- Brick 1 high-value search-intent coverage
- the eight-point `What should parents look for in the best online phonics classes?` framework
- the Tiny Steps evidence bridge to curriculum, class samples, testimonials and pricing
- AEO FAQ coverage introduced by Brick 1
- `#phonics-class-quality-criteria` ItemList structured data
- SEO title/description/canonical contracts
- the boundary against unsupported `#1` / objectively-best-provider claims

## Programme-fact consistency

The public Phonics age range is **ages 3–12**.

The landing page must not show an `ages 3–10` primary-pathway statement. Hero, visible facts and course schema stay aligned to ages 3–12.

## Final scrolling psychology / parent narrative

The page should read as one guided decision story:

1. **Orient me** — Hero + quick answer explain what Tiny Steps Phonics is and the broad reading journey.
2. **Help me recognise my child** — The single `Who this page is for` section owns parent problems and programme fit.
3. **Teach me how to judge quality** — Preserve the Brick 1 eight-point parent comparison framework in full.
4. **Show me proof** — Parent feedback follows the evidence-led comparison framework instead of another repeated feature grid.
5. **Show me how teaching works** — The protected responsive-teaching section owns modelling, observation, correction and retry behaviour.
6. **Show me the actual programme** — Ages 3–12, live 1:1 format, duration, outcomes and three inspectable curriculum levels.
7. **Show me the roadmap** — Foundation-to-advanced stage progression is owned by the learning-path section.
8. **Show me how progress is seen** — Parent-visible checkpoints and realistic progress framing.
9. **Help me compare alternatives** — Apps, school reading, home reading and live guided phonics are compared only after the programme is understood.
10. **Confirm availability** — India-wide delivery appears later in the journey rather than interrupting early programme comprehension.
11. **Let me explore without losing the story** — One consolidated resource hub for the research guide, free practice, topic guides and related support.
12. **Answer remaining objections** — One canonical FAQ accordion, not a second repeated FAQ block.
13. **Make the next step feel safe** — Assessment process, what parents receive and a no-pressure final CTA.

## One-owner-per-idea rule

Each major idea gets one primary visible owner.

| Idea | Primary owner | Removed / consolidated overlap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parent problems / programme fit | `#problems` | Removed the later `Who should join Tiny Steps phonics classes?` block and folded the separate uncertainty CTA into this section |
| Best-class selection criteria | `#best-phonics-classes` | Brick 1 section remains intact |
| Tiny Steps proof | Brick 1 evidence bridge + parent feedback | Removed a second `Why Tiny Steps phonics is different` feature grid that repeated Brick 1 criteria |
| Teacher methodology | `#teacher-delivery` | Removed the separate `Tiny Steps Phonics Method` sequence that repeated learning-path and teaching concepts |
| Curriculum facts / outcomes | `#program` | Removed the extra `What your child learns` grid; programme facts + level tabs now own this information |
| Stage roadmap | `#learning-path` | Keeps stage progression separate from programme facts |
| Parent progress | `#progress` | Keeps progress framing in one location |
| Alternative support comparison | apps/school/home/live comparison section | Appears once, after programme and progress |
| FAQs | `#faq` | Removed the earlier duplicate four-question FAQ card grid |
| Resources | `#resources` | Merged the former research/practice block and lower resource directories; buyer-guide CTA is not repeated again here because Brick 1 already owns that handoff |
| Assessment journey | `What happens next?` + final `#assessment` CTA | CTA repetition is deliberate; explanatory content is not repeated elsewhere |

## Visual direction

- Preserve the current orange / sky / navy Tiny Steps visual language.
- Use fewer nested white cards inside white cards.
- Let whitespace, typography and deliberate anchor sections create hierarchy instead of adding copy.
- Keep card grids only where comparison or scanning materially benefits the parent.
- Tighten global section padding and shadows without making the page visually dense.
- Keep sticky navigation horizontally scroll-safe on smaller screens.
- Keep primary CTA hierarchy obvious and avoid adding more floating UI.

## Acceptance checks

- Brick 1 regression tests remain unchanged and green.
- No `3–10` Phonics age claim remains on `/phonics`.
- No duplicate full `Who is this for` / `Who should join` information block.
- No duplicate `Why Tiny Steps` feature grid repeating Brick 1 criteria.
- No duplicate `Tiny Steps Phonics Method` sequence repeating the learning roadmap / teacher-delivery model.
- No separate `What your child learns` card grid repeating programme outcomes.
- No duplicate four-question FAQ block before the canonical FAQ accordion.
- Resource discovery is consolidated without deleting important topic or related-program routes.
- The eight Brick 1 quality criteria remain visible and machine-readable.
- The page still exposes curriculum, class samples, testimonials, pricing and buyer-guide evidence routes.
- Responsive teaching remains present for B14 trust/methodology guardrails.
- The final assessment flow remains explicit and no-pressure.
