# Phonics landing-page narrative polish

## Objective

Improve `/phonics` as a premium, parent-first landing journey without weakening or rewriting the protected Phonics Brick 1 SEO/AEO/GEO authority contract.

The optimisation is consolidation and presentation work, not a new SEO expansion.

## Protected baseline

Do not disturb:

- `/phonics` as the main programme/authority owner
- `/best-online-phonics-classes-for-kids-in-india` as the buyer/comparison owner
- Brick 1 high-value search-intent coverage
- the eight-point `What should parents look for in the best online phonics classes?` framework
- the Tiny Steps evidence bridge to curriculum, class samples, testimonials and pricing
- AEO FAQ coverage introduced by Brick 1
- `#phonics-class-quality-criteria` ItemList structured data
- SEO title/description/canonical contracts
- the boundary against unsupported `#1` / objectively-best-provider claims

## Programme-fact consistency

The public Phonics age range is **ages 3–12**.

The landing page must not show an `ages 3–10` primary-pathway statement. Hero, visible facts and course schema should remain aligned to ages 3–12.

## Scrolling psychology / parent narrative

The final page should read as one guided story:

1. **Orient me** — What is Tiny Steps Phonics and what is the reading journey?
2. **Help me recognise my child** — Which reading problems or parent concerns fit this page?
3. **Teach me how to judge quality** — Preserve the Brick 1 eight-point parent comparison framework.
4. **Show me why Tiny Steps is relevant** — Differentiators and inspectable evidence, not unsupported claims.
5. **Show me how the teaching works** — Tiny Steps method and responsive teacher delivery.
6. **Show me what my child will learn** — Concrete skills and programme structure.
7. **Show me the roadmap** — Foundation → Early → Advanced / stage-based learning path.
8. **Show me how progress is seen** — Parent-visible checkpoints and realistic progress framing.
9. **Help me compare alternatives** — Apps, school reading, home reading and live guided phonics.
10. **Give me proof and broader availability** — Parent feedback and India-wide delivery without keyword stuffing.
11. **Let me explore without losing the story** — One consolidated resource area for parent guides, free practice and related routes.
12. **Answer remaining objections** — One canonical FAQ area, not a second repeated FAQ block.
13. **Make the next step feel safe** — Assessment process, what parents receive, no-pressure final CTA.

## One-owner-per-idea rule

Each major idea gets one primary visible owner.

| Idea | Primary owner | Remove / repurpose overlap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parent problems / programme fit | `#problems` | Do not repeat the same audience list later as another full `Who should join` section |
| Best-class selection criteria | `#best-phonics-classes` | Brick 1 section remains intact |
| Brand differentiators | `Why Tiny Steps phonics is different` | Keep distinct from the generic selection criteria |
| Teaching sequence | `The Tiny Steps Phonics Method` | Avoid restating the same sequence in generic marketing copy |
| Curriculum facts | `#program` | Keep age, format, duration, structure and level tabs here |
| Stage roadmap | `#learning-path` | Keep stage progression here |
| Teacher behaviour | `#teacher-delivery` | Keep modelling/correction/retry detail here |
| Parent progress | measurable-progress section | Do not re-list generic programme features as progress metrics |
| FAQs | `#faq` | Remove the earlier duplicate four-question FAQ card grid |
| Resources | one consolidated resource area | Avoid separate buyer-guide/practice directories that repeat the same links |
| Assessment journey | `What happens next?` + final `#assessment` CTA | CTA repetition is allowed; informational repetition is not |

## Visual direction

- Preserve the current orange / sky / navy Tiny Steps visual language.
- Use fewer nested white cards inside white cards.
- Let major sections alternate between light editorial space and deliberate dark anchor sections.
- Increase hierarchy through whitespace, typography and section transitions rather than adding more copy.
- Keep card grids only where comparison or scanning materially benefits the parent.
- Maintain clear mobile stacking and horizontal-scroll safety for sticky navigation.
- Prevent floating UI from visually competing with primary page CTAs.

## Acceptance checks

- Brick 1 regression tests remain unchanged and green.
- No `3–10` Phonics age claim remains on `/phonics`.
- No duplicate full `Who is this for` / `Who should join` information block.
- No duplicate four-question FAQ block before the canonical FAQ accordion.
- Resource discovery is consolidated without deleting important internal routes.
- The eight Brick 1 quality criteria remain visible and machine-readable.
- The page still exposes curriculum, class samples, testimonials, pricing and buyer-guide evidence routes.
- The final assessment flow remains explicit and no-pressure.
