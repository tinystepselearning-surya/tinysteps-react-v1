# Tiny Steps Parent Story Consent & Snippet Bank

Canonical domain: https://tinystepslearning.com

## Purpose
This document helps Tiny Steps collect, approve, and organize parent-approved story snippets for testimonials, digital PR, media pitches, case-study style content, and AI-search trust building.

## Important Privacy Rule
Do not publish a child’s name, photo, video, class recording, voice, school name, location, or personal learning details unless explicit written consent is documented. When unsure, anonymize.

## Safe Story Positioning
Stories should focus on learning journey, parent observations, teaching approach, confidence, participation, and progress signals. Avoid guaranteed outcomes, medical/diagnostic claims, rankings, or exaggerated promises.

## Safe Anonymized Snippet Examples
- One parent shared that a 5-year-old learner could identify letters but needed help connecting sounds into words. After guided blending practice, the parent noticed the child becoming more willing to try short words during reading time.
- One parent observed that a child was hesitant to read aloud in class. With structured phonics practice and teacher prompts, the child started participating more consistently in short reading tasks.
- A parent reported that the child understood basic grammar terms but struggled to apply them in writing. After guided examples and correction, the parent noticed clearer sentence use in school homework.
- One parent said the child often wrote incomplete thoughts. With step-by-step sentence formation practice, the child began writing fuller, clearer sentences with better confidence.
- A parent shared that the child was quiet during communication activities at first. Through picture talk prompts and repeated speaking tasks, the child began responding in longer sentences.
- One parent observed that pronunciation errors were affecting the child’s speaking confidence. With regular teacher-led practice and calm correction, the child became more comfortable speaking in class.
- A parent noted that home practice felt confusing before enrollment. After receiving progress updates and next-step guidance, the parent felt clearer about what to reinforce between classes.
- One parent mentioned that the child enjoyed visual activities and interactive tasks more than worksheet-only practice. Over time, the parent noticed stronger participation and better focus during live sessions.

## Channel-Specific Usage Rules
### Website Testimonials
- Use parent-approved text only.
- Keep child identity private unless consent allows.
- Do not add ratings unless collected and verified.

### Digital PR / Guest Articles
- Use anonymized learning examples.
- Link to the most relevant page.
- Avoid sounding like an advertisement.

### Social Media
- Use only approved visuals/videos.
- Avoid showing class recordings without consent.
- Keep captions educational and parent-friendly.

### Class Samples
- Use only recordings with documented permission.
- Blur/remove private information where needed.
- Confirm consent before reuse.

## Review Checklist Before Publishing
- Consent documented
- Child identity protected
- Parent quote approved
- No guaranteed result
- No ranking claim
- No private information exposed
- Correct page linked
- Final approval completed

## Monthly Maintenance
- Review consent status monthly.
- Remove or pause expired/unclear stories.
- Ask parents for renewed permission before reusing old stories.
- Update approved story snippets after major learning milestones.
