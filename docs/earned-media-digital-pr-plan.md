# Tiny Steps Earned Media & Digital PR Plan

Canonical domain: https://tinystepslearning.com

## Purpose
This plan helps Tiny Steps earn credible third-party mentions, parent trust signals, and AI-search visibility through ethical outreach, useful education content, and real parent/teacher stories.

## Why This Matters for AI Search
AI search engines often rely on third-party mentions, trusted publications, expert content, and consistent brand references. Tiny Steps should not depend only on its own website. It should build credible external signals over time.

## Safe Brand Positioning
- Tiny Steps Learning is a premium online English learning school for children.
- Core programs include phonics, grammar, sentence formation, communication, and public speaking.
- Classes are delivered online in one-on-one and small-group formats.
- Teaching focuses on structured learning, child-friendly activities, guided correction, and parent progress updates.
- Do not use unsupported “best,” “#1,” or ranking claims.

## Target Outreach Categories
1. Parent blogs and parenting websites  
   Look for family education blogs, parenting newsletters, and practical home-learning sites.
2. Education blogs and child learning websites  
   Look for phonics, early literacy, grammar, and child skill-building publishers.
3. Local Hyderabad / India digital media  
   Look for city education features, family lifestyle portals, and regional learning coverage.
4. Teacher communities and educator newsletters  
   Look for teacher-led publications, classroom strategy newsletters, and educator groups.
5. Mom creators and parent micro-influencers  
   Look for parent creators sharing real learning routines and child development experiences.
6. Podcasts about parenting, early learning, reading, or child confidence  
   Look for hosts covering practical child learning support and parent decision-making.
7. Online learning directories  
   Look for reputable listing platforms that allow clear program descriptions and links.
8. NRI / Indian parents abroad communities  
   Look for diaspora parent forums, expat family groups, and NRI child-learning communities.

## Quality Rules
- Use only real parent stories with permission.
- Do not publish child names/photos/videos without proper consent.
- Do not claim guaranteed results.
- Do not claim rankings or awards unless verified.
- Prefer useful educational content over sales-heavy promotion.
- Link to the most relevant Tiny Steps page, not always the homepage.
- Track every earned mention.

## Monthly Rhythm
- Week 1: identify 20 outreach targets.
- Week 2: send 10 personalized pitches.
- Week 3: follow up and offer expert quotes.
- Week 4: record mentions, update AI visibility tracker, and plan next topics.
