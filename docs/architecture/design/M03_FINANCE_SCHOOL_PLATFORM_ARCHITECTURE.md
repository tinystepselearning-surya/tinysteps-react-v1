## 1. D08 — Commerce and Finance

Separate academic offering from commercial offer and effective agreement. Separate cash receipt, earned service charge, allocated amount, wallet event, teacher entitlement, actual cash payout, noncash offset/withholding and reporting. Use current ledger authority and existing IDs; do not replace current working billing with a speculative accounting system.

| COF CommercialOffer | commercialOffers/{offerId} | Effective version of price/terms for product/approved delivery |

Source index.ts exports sessionRevenue.onSessionRevenueWrite, not legacy revenue.ts equivalent. Current billingCharges and teacherEarnings use sessionId for normal session; demo earnings use distinct deterministic IDs. Preserve finance cycle and snapshots (billingRateSnapshot, teacherPayRateSnapshot, financialTermsCurrency, financialTermsSnapshotVersion), source service day, service month, accrual marker and earned status. No posting from virtual occurrence, mere Teams evidence, demo as ordinary class, daily school attendance, or observation. School recurring tuition/one-time fees require separately approved commercial obligation schedule; do not translate all physical-school days into per-class fees. Group pricing and teacher earning split require explicit business policy, verified participant/staff grain and financial parity; no historical split inferred.

Money contract: target safe integer minor units + ISO currency, exact signed semantics (charge/receipt/refund/adjustment defined per type), explicit source precision and rounding rule; preserve existing number-valued source records and original amounts/provenance. Decimal conversion must reject ambiguous currency/precision, out-of-range or nonrepresentable values rather than round silently. No dual authoritative amounts. Distinguish billed vs received, service period vs receipt/payment date, gross entitlement vs cash transferred vs offset/withholding. Preserve paid/settled/manual-void evidence; correction flag/review for settled or unsafe changes. Wallet signed event sums plus verified opening balance reconcile current balance; retain ledger, do not reconstruct only from currentBalance. Separate recordPayment and adminReceiveParentPayment routes, deterministic replay and charge allocation, top-up and deduction/reversal cycles; no payment double-counted as both receipt and top-up. Deductions asynchronous and revision-aware, not cash. Payout V2 earningMonthKey, paidAt and paymentMonthKey remain distinct; preserve offsets and actual cash date. Reconciliation per tenant/payer/currency/period and teacher/earning period compares counts, sums, allocation, wallet balances, cash/noncash and orphan links; do not equate same monthly labels. Legacy payment confirmations and configuration consumers remain unresolved. Discounts, taxes/GST, refund, package/credits and physical fee policy approved separately, no new policy invented.

Logical commands: publishOffer, acceptAgreement, issueEntitlement (Commerce); accrueChargeAndEarning (existing eligibility+idempotent transaction), recordReceipt, allocateReceipt, appendWalletEvent, correctCharge, recordPayout, reconcileFinanceCase (Finance). Exact atomic transaction boundaries preserve current writer/trigger coordination; external payment and bank transfer not atomic with Firestore. Use replay key and payload conflict checks, source revision, bounded scope, reconciliation and explicit reversal. No general direct client ledger writes. Finance role/guardian own approved account statements/teacher own earning view only; no unrelated payroll or bank details. Views use existing monthly projections and incremental/recompute paths, not repeated full-ledger reads. No new duplicate finance rollup or automatic scheduled reconciliation activated.

CRM rule from owner direction: same parent/child same-day repeat collapse accidental duplicate; new-day enquiry is fresh work including previously not-interested/enrolled as applicable. Preserve source first/last inquiry times, canonical root, inquiry history and existing identity/dedupe keys; source dedupe uses normalized phone and child name, NOT Person identity proof. Review actual same-day/different-day edge cases and existing demo retry/unique keys before any change; not assert source perfectly implements owner policy. Demo attempt lifecycle and reschedule creates new attempt, assignment/completing teacher and conversion corrections preserved; conversion not automatically enrollment, no duplicate demo or earnings. Retain deliberate admin delete feature and its linked-record/history behavior, not generic erase or silent cascade. Prospective campaign/admission assessment/recommendation capture only with approval. Preserve operational admin workflows and staff task visibility.

Workforce: role access separate from employment eligibility, teaching assignment and session staff. Local B2B teacher roster may not be global person; verified linking only. Preserve current private staff profile restrictions, training/review evidence. Student success follow-up distinct from CRM and support; no AI-created observations or punitive automation. Physical school uses D01 guardian, M01 campus/institutional placement, M02 daily attendance, D08 commercial agreements. Preschool pickup, safeguarding, emergency information and consent use narrowly authorized roles and minimal capture; no automatic consent from primary guardian or household; validate identity/purpose, effective period, revocation, custody/access policy and explicit handover. Preserve current B2B school principal read-only and aggregate boundaries; owned preschool management capabilities separately approved. Retention/legal policy requires jurisdiction-specific review; no legal compliance certification in this design.

Messaging source sends message, unread/notification state in transaction and provider push separately; preserve existing phone-number restriction, scoped participant membership and notifications; distinguish accepted intent, attempted, delivered (provider evidence only), failed/unknown. Do not treat message ID or clientMessageId alone as proven dedupe; verify replay contracts. Token hash keys and raw token restricted. No scheduled push resurrection, no external message sent. Support access purpose-bound and audited, no general cross-tenant browsing.

Publish only approved version, parent-specific scope and audit.

## 4. Migration, cost, security and verification

Design target records first; classify REUSE/EXTEND/EXTRACT/NEW. Existing same Firestore database default. New collection candidates are not asserted empty, collision checks and exact schema/keys/references required before activation. M04 resolve all outstanding typed field shapes, source aliases, access/retention, lifecycle transition matrices, exact query/index contracts and per-workflow read/write budgets. Existing IDs/history/financial numbers/contact/consent preserved; no unnecessary historical migration or inferred data; missing capabilities capture prospectively through approved workflows. No cross-tenant linking or public data exposure. All new operational roots tenant scoped; existing platform-admin aggregates stay platform restricted, never reused as tenant view. Financial/staff/safety/provider data restricted; no generic client writes. Actual effective Rules including overlapping recursive allow clauses and Admin SDK guards independently tested. Least privilege, no logging raw sensitive values. 
Migration per affected family: recoverability+restore evidence, source mapping and bounded dry run, provenance, idempotent backfill and conflict quarantine, semantic and financial reconciliation, shadow/canary read switch, distinct writer handoff, rollback preserving post-cutover writes, observe then explicit retire. Preserve all current functions/consumers until replacement parity. No automatic writes from historical imports or new schema mere presence.
Acceptance scenarios (design, not executed): repeat receipt/charge/earning/payout no duplicate; settled correction review; currency/precision mismatch rejected; wallet events and opening reconcile; partial allocation/refund and cash vs offset; service month vs actual cash date; demo vs academic entitlement; same-day duplicate and different-day returning inquiry; rescheduled demo and conversion correction; deliberate deletion preserves applicable history; tenant/guardian/principal/teacher denied unrelated scope; staff-private and health records inaccessible to general UI; withdrawn consent and pickup authority recheck; duplicate/out-of-order notification/outbox replay; provider failure and recording sharing; immutable report and evidence linkage; analytics missing vs zero and day-boundary failure retention; approved recovery drill, read costs, native/mobile and regression gates. 
M03 is initial coverage of remaining packages, not all business contracts complete. M04 is mandatory integrated closure across 18 domains and shared foundations, including D01 profile detail, source/live verification gaps, independent reviewer sign-off, executable design/ADR and affected phase gates. P01 acceptance, P02/P03/P04/M3 and production gates unchanged. No production data, functions, rules, CI, deployments, automation or external provider changes.
