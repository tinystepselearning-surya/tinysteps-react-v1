**Status: proposed product design; not executable contract adoption or runtime implementation.** Owner directs return to product architecture while live infrastructure/security review is pending. R62 and R59/R61 engineering evidence remain accepted; P01/P02/P03, M3 and R2/R3 implementation gates remain held. ## 1. Product model: one platform, different institutional contexts

Use the current default Firestore for Tiny Steps-owned online and physical operations by default. Tenant is the customer/security boundary; Organisation is the operating institution; Campus is an optional physical location, not a delivery format. A B2B partnership is an explicit service relationship, not common ownership. Independent SaaS customer isolation/deployment strategy requires separate decision before onboarding.

Illustrative relationships (labels only, not actual identifiers, mapping or records):

| Context | Tenant | Organisation | Campus | Delivery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Owned online school | Tiny Steps | Online operation | Not required | Premium 1:1 direct enrollment/teacher assignment; small group explicit cohort |
| Owned preschool | Tiny Steps | Preschool operation | Physical location | Institutional placement + academic year/class, daily attendance |
| Future owned second location | Tiny Steps | Approved owning organisation | Second location | Same contracts; scope separately |
| Partner school | Explicitly mapped customer boundary | Partner institution | Only verified locations | Existing B2B relationship, current schoolAdmin reader and assigned LP management |
| Future independent SaaS school | Independent tenant | Customer institution | Its own locations | No Tiny Steps customer-data access by default |

No inference that these conceptual organisations or tenant relationships exist in production. Map from authoritative business evidence only. One verified Person can have separately authorized memberships; no shared visibility merely because a person is linked to two institutions. A tenant/organisation cannot be supplied as trusted by the client. Maintain same domain owners for enrollment, attendance and finance; physical school attendance must not trigger online session charges.

## 3. Proposed architectural decision — extend logical ROL authority, compatibility first

M01 left CTX (a new contextual grant store) unresolved. Prefer one logical ROL authority with explicit versioning and a successor capability policy catalog. Do not allocate a CTX record or duplicate the same authorization fact in two independently writable stores. Physical storage decision is conditional on reader compatibility.

**Concrete source blocker:** loadCanonicalAuthAccessInput queries ALL roleAssignments for personId up to 51 and parses each. readRoleAssignment accepts only existing CanonicalRole and scopeType global/organisation/household; tenant/campus or unknown roles cause the whole read-model refresh to fail. Similarly the v1 membership reducer ignores effectiveStartAt/effectiveEndAt. A new schoolAdmin organisation assignment using the old shape could grant old school-reader access irrespective of new tenant/campus restrictions. Therefore neither in-place enum extension nor writing a superficially compatible v2 row into the current collection is safe without reviewing and migrating every affected reader/writer/sampler/Rules path. Adding fields that v1 ignores is not safe authorization enforcement. Current canonical principal parser has no model age/source revision enforcement. These are observed source constraints, not proven production incidents.

Proposed successor semantic envelope (draft names; no allocation): assignment identity, verified personId, scope discriminant tenant/organisation/campus (platform access separately reviewed), explicit tenant/organisation/campus components matching actual parents, versioned policy-role key, status, effectiveFrom/effectiveUntil, revision and provenance. Role templates resolve to versioned named capabilities; reject unknown role, action, scope or revision. No arbitrary caller-provided capability array; deny-by-default; no implicit wildcard/inheritance. Require current explicit eligibility and relationship separately. Use half-open interval [start,end); server clock; open end only where reviewed. Changes to role policy are governed versions, never retroactively reinterpret old grants. Existing global roles and household scope retained in existing authority until their specific handoff.

Migration stages:
1. Pin current readers/writers and ledger; catalogue all ROL/MEM consumers and triggers (including v1 projection, bounded sampler, admin guards, legacy Rules, migrations, reports and clients). Confirm exact old behavior and caps. No existing assignment mutations.
2. Draft version-aware reader partition and compatibility tests. Old paths must see exactly legacy-valid records and retain current rights; new paths must never interpret legacy role labels as new privileges. If safe same-collection partition cannot be proven, design separately versioned storage for disjoint new capabilities, with single writer per logical grant and explicit authority routing, no duplicated facts. No storage adoption until reviewed ADR and compatibility.
3. After separately authorized isolated implementation, verify legacy old/new reader behavior and new two-tenant positive/negative fixtures, revocation fences, bounds and source version drift. Establish exact Rules, SDK, callable, cache, provider and job boundaries.
4. Only after required live gates, activate narrowly scoped new capability paths behind disabled-by-default feature controls. Verify effective current principal and exact owner mapping; no blanket role backfill or global Rules switch. Retain old authority until each path is reconciled. Rollback disables new route and preserves any separately authorized new facts; not blind Git revert.

## 4. Product command and access design

Proposed action vocabulary for review; not existing permissions or allocated registry entries.

| Action | Required context and relationship | Explicit denial / constraint |
|---|---|---|
| institution.create / campus.create | Separately approved platform or tenant institutional administrator; verified owning tenant/org | Partner principal, parent, teacher not permitted by default |
| institution.manageRoster | Future owned-school operator with explicit org/campus capability; active learner placement relationship | Current schoolAdmin remains reader; other tenant/campus denied |
| attendance.markSchoolDaily | Explicit assigned school attendance role for exact register and active placement | No online billing mutation; no mark for unrelated child |
| attendance.markOnlineSession | Existing teacher participation/assignment authority; exact session learner and current business rule | No broader roster rights; no inferred right from membership |
| learner.readTeachingView | Active approved assignment and minimum fields for exact learner/group/session | No unrelated finance or safeguarding access |
| learner.readGuardianView | Verified current guardian relationship plus reviewed purpose/fields and consent/custody policy | Household member/payer alone denied; no other sibling unless own authorization |
| finance.readApprovedView / finance.post | Separate role/capability and finance scope; current action state and authoritative ledger for write | Teacher/principal role not blanket finance access; reports do not authorize transaction |
| partner.readSchool / partner.manageAssignedSchool | Preserve current schoolReader/Manager exact existing checks until successor parity; new scope requires approved BPT/OAS/ROL mapping | No tenant-wide read by B2B relationship or automatic owned-school writes |
| platform.supportScoped | Separately approved audited support case, exact tenant/actions, time-limited grant and current operator | No standing cross-tenant customer visibility from role name |

Command input: authenticated caller derived server-side, requested scope and exact target (untrusted until resolved), operationId, expected revision, typed payload. For new institution/create/update commands, resolve authoritative owner, eligibility, grant and resource relation before execution; transact re-read required authority revisions and target invariants together with business mutation and idempotency marker. Same operation ID and same canonical payload return existing outcome only after authorization; changed payload conflicts. Revoked actor cannot replay to retrieve sensitive outcome. Scope/revision mismatch denies or returns conflict without write. Async jobs carry explicit scope and verified service authority, recheck current authority before side effects. Audit minimal action/decision/revision without child contents. Exact transaction read/write limits, actor capabilities, lifecycle transitions and audit retention need review before code.

## 5. Read-efficient and safe current authorization

Tier 1: current auth status, revocation, tenant state, capability, relationship, transaction/current attendance and operations. Tier 2: nonurgent prior-closed-day management, teacher, parent and finance reports (existing T-1 policy). Report watermark never authorizes access; current context checked independently. No missing-report raw scan fallback.

Propose bounded point lookups for new actions: one current principal anchor plus exact tenant eligibility, tenant status, optional org/campus status, exact effective grant and target/relationship. Initial design ceiling for a straightforward campus scoped authorization path: 1 principal + 1 current revocation/version fence (may be merged only if proven atomic) + 1 tenant + 1 membership + 1 organisation + 1 campus + 1 selected grant + 1 target + 1 exact domain relationship = 9 logical point operations before business payload. This is a proposed worst-path design budget, not measured reads, billed cost, implementation or a compulsory nine reads for every action. Tenant-only/online paths omit irrelevant org/campus lookups; direct premium 1:1 never requires group/placement reads. Existing paths unchanged; v1 school reader currently principal lookup + school lookup (plus downstream reads). Avoid scanning all memberships or all grants on each request; deterministic exact keys/approved selected context only. For multi-grant cases define bounded policy resolution separately, fail closed on overflow. Evaluate safe co-located projections with source revisions; never remove necessary current checks solely for cost. High-risk commands read authority fences inside write transaction; asynchronous rebuild can only narrow/deny until verified revision current; no stale allow on revocation. No arbitrary cache TTL promised. Measure Rules-dependent reads, query/index entries, retries and payload separately during approved verification.

## 6. First product acceptance matrix (proposed)

- Owned online direct 1:1: correct current tenant/organisation and teacher assignment allowed; no group/campus prerequisite; unassigned teacher and different tenant denied; multiple enrollments remain separate.
- Owned preschool: correct organisation/campus scoped roster operator allowed for explicitly permitted action; other campus, unrelated learner, inactive membership, suspended tenant and unknown policy denied; daily attendance separated from online finance.
- Partner school: v1 reader still read-only and nonarchived; LP still assigned-school only; role extension never leaks access to another partner or owned preschool; preserve v1 admin behavior for existing paths.
- Guardian: verified allowed child only; unrelated child, household-only, payer-only, ended/restricted relationship, guessed identifier and revoked grant denied.
- Identity lifecycle: disabled auth, suspended person, stale/missing/wrong-revision projection, expired interval, malformed context, conflicting owner and unsupported role deny. Revocation racing command must not commit after authority fence changes. No cached allow after revocation.
- Compatibility: old loader accepts all prior valid fixture shapes and rejects unknown/malformed; no new tenant/campus grants appear in old source lane until approved partition; source caps and role/organisation intersection unchanged. New resolver may not grant on v1 label alone. Verify deterministic key collision and no legacy key rewrite.
- Jobs/reports/exports/links: same context and field boundaries, current auth independent of T-1 watermark, no cross-tenant export or restore, no raw fallback; direct client Rules and server SDK separately verified.
