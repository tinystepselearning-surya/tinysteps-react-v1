## Findings and binding design clarifications
These clarifications supersede conflicting draft wording in M01/M02 for review, not adopted schema or current behavior.

| ID | Finding | Resolution in design / registry | Remaining gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| X01 | Tenant-bound household membership had no tenant field; relationship alone could be misconstrued as access. | HMB gets proposed tenantId, verified scope/relationship checks; no backfill or automatic access. Existing global identity records are not blindly tenant-stamped. | D02 policy and household rollout held. |
| X02 | Offering can share catalogue definitions but must own delivery scope. Nested school IDs and unit IDs are not global. | Preserve DLV tenantId, catalogue owner policy, contextual composite reference resolution; no guessed global keys or implicit cross-customer sharing. | M04 exact composite key schema and scope tests. |

Preserve virtual calendar and existing authorized on-demand attendance/ad hoc writes; do not revive old generators. | Runtime regression and deployed parity. |
| X05 | A group recording is one provider asset, potentially accessible to multiple verified learners. ## Scope and disposition
Design defects above are addressed by this review plus linked registry revision and M03 boundary contracts. Existing registry item identifiers are preserved; X05 and X07 deliberately revise proposed field/ownership semantics. These are proposals only. No claim of perfect architecture or production readiness. Outstanding contract details, approved live data, exact IAM/Rules and recovery evidence, independent sign-off, source drift and implementation gates remain open. They block executable adoption/migration, not preparation of downstream design material for independent review under the existing design-only approach. Do not interpret this as P01/P02/P03/P04 or M3 approval. M03 design may proceed on the corrected assumptions; M04 must resolve residuals and review all 18 business domains and shared foundations. No runtime changes, live operations, CI run or fresh regression testing.
