# Tiny Steps SEO Recovery — Brick 13 GSC Measurement Framework

**Date:** 2026-09-12  
**Status:** ✅ CLOSED — recovery measurement framework established  
**Master reference:** `docs/TINY_STEPS_SEO_RECOVERY_MASTER_PLAN_2026-09-12.md`

## Objective

Stop judging SEO recovery by one headline traffic number.

Brick 13 separates the recovery into three measurement systems:

1. **Revenue SEO** — search demand that can lead to a programme, comparison, fee or assessment decision.
2. **Qualified Informational SEO** — parent-problem and learning-stage searches that can assist a later commercial decision.
3. **Traffic SEO** — high-volume free-resource/discovery searches that are valuable, but must not be allowed to hide deterioration in commercial intent.

The framework combines finalized Google Search Console evidence with first-touch website lead attribution supplied from the Tiny Steps marketing dashboard.

## 6. Attribution rules

Brick 13 explicitly prevents channel mixing.

- GSC measures **Google Search** visibility and clicks.
- Google Organic first-touch leads can be connected to SEO business outcomes.
- `chatgpt.com`, Direct, Referral, WhatsApp, Other campaign and Legacy/unattributed are reported separately.
- ChatGPT referrals must not be counted as Google Organic simply because both ultimately reach the same website.
- Landing-page outcomes are useful, but they are not proof that a particular Google query caused the lead unless query-level attribution exists.

## 7. Decision rules

A recovery win requires more than higher impressions.

### Revenue SEO win

Look for a combination of:

- stable or improving intended-owner rankings;
- improving commercial clicks/CTR;
- qualified Google Organic leads remaining healthy or increasing;
- demo progression remaining healthy;
- admissions evaluated only after cohort maturity.

### Qualified informational win

Look for:

- correct authority page ranking for its query family;
- clicks/CTR improving without ownership drift;
- useful onward engagement or assisted lead contribution where measurable.

### Traffic SEO win

Look for:

- protected traffic and visibility;
- no technical/indexing loss;
- no cannibalization of programme/comparison owners.

Traffic SEO cannot compensate for Revenue SEO deterioration.

---

## 8. Review cadence

- **7 days after deployment:** technical/indexation/deployment verification.
- **14–21 days:** preliminary query/page movement review.
- **28+ days:** full like-for-like GSC recovery review.
- **Monthly after cohort maturity:** business attribution review for leads, demos and admissions.

Use finalized GSC windows only. Avoid reacting to one or two days of volatility.

## Definition of done

Brick 13 is closed because Tiny Steps now has a formal recovery measurement system that answers three different questions independently:

**Are commercial searches recovering?**  
**Are useful parent/problem searches strengthening the right authority pages?**  
**Are high-volume free resources growing without masking commercial weakness?**

Business attribution is layered on top without mis-crediting ChatGPT or other non-Google channels to Organic Search.
