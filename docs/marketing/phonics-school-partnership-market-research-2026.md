# Tiny Steps school phonics partnership: market and pricing research

Research date: 1 August 2026  
Primary market: private, offline, international, CBSE, ICSE, Cambridge, and IB schools in India  
Secondary market: international schools outside India that can use remote teacher training and support

## Executive conclusion

The school-market opportunity is not another phonics teacher certificate or a library of downloadable worksheets. The defensible Tiny Steps offer is a campus licence that combines:

1. a structured phonics scope and sequence;
2. classroom-ready teaching and practice content;
3. live training for the school's existing teachers;
4. assessment and reteaching guidance;
5. coaching and implementation reviews across the academic year; and
6. clear campus-level usage rights.

Public international benchmarks range from roughly £400 to £899 per year for school phonics memberships and bundles. More comprehensive or international implementations frequently use bespoke quotes. Indian providers often publish individual teacher-training fees or sell materials, while institutional programmes generally use enquiry-led pricing.

That creates room for transparent, premium-but-accessible India pricing with a low-risk pilot and a more valuable whole-school plan.

## Recommended Tiny Steps pricing architecture

### 1. Eight-week school pilot — ₹24,900 + GST

- One selected grade
- Up to 4 teachers
- Up to 60 learners
- Selected curriculum block and classroom resources
- Teacher onboarding and rehearsal
- Baseline and end-of-pilot review
- Pilot fee credited to an annual plan if upgraded within 30 days

Why: reduces procurement risk and creates the evidence needed for a larger adoption decision.

### 2. Focused Stage Licence — ₹59,000 + GST per campus per academic year

- One phonics pathway
- Up to 5 teachers and 150 learners
- Two live training labs
- Classroom content and internal print rights
- Monthly implementation office hour
- Two progress-review cycles

Why: sits above low-cost content and certificate products while remaining accessible to a school testing one age band.

### 3. Whole-School Phonics Partnership — ₹99,000 + GST per campus per academic year

- Full foundation-to-advanced pathway
- Up to 12 teachers and 400 learners
- Four live training and rehearsal labs
- Teaching, practice, and assessment resources
- Monthly coaching and priority support
- Three observation and leadership reviews

Why: this is the recommended plan. It is comparable to international membership benchmarks but adds India-context implementation and high-touch live support.

### 4. Multi-Campus Partnership — from ₹1.75 lakh + GST per academic year

- Up to 3 campuses in the base scope
- Up to 25 teachers and 900 learners
- Central coordinator enablement
- Shared rollout and reporting framework
- Campus-level reviews
- Custom expansion and renewal plan

Why: school networks need a governance and training layer, not simply multiplied logins.

## Positioning and messaging decisions

- Lead with “your teachers deliver; Tiny Steps trains, equips, and supports them.”
- Use “phonics program for schools in India” as the primary search phrase.
- Describe the offer as an annual campus partnership and content licence, not outsourced classes.
- State transparent starting prices and show the teacher/learner limits.
- Keep multi-campus and overseas pricing proposal-led.
- Do not claim DfE validation, government approval, IB alignment, Cambridge alignment, or affiliation unless formally obtained.
- Use “board-agnostic” and “complements the existing English timetable.”
- Avoid numerical student-outcome promises until Tiny Steps completes controlled pilots and has permission to publish school case studies.
- Explain the licence: annual, non-transferable, limited to agreed campuses, teachers, learners, and content scope.

## India policy context

The NCERT National Curriculum Framework for the Foundational Stage describes phonological awareness, sound-symbol association, decoding, comprehension, and writing as connected foundational literacy skills. Tiny Steps can accurately say its pathway teaches these skills. It should not imply official approval or accreditation.
