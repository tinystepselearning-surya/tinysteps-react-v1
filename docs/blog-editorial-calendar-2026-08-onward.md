# Tiny Steps Monthly Blog Editorial Calendar — August 2026 Onward

## Decision

Tiny Steps will move from the old weekly blog model to a **monthly evergreen blog model**.

The weekly series is no longer the right publishing structure because:

- the numbered weekly campaign already has a large historical gap;
- most older `week-*` posts are intentionally treated as noindex archive content;
- the strongest current SEO direction is evergreen parent-intent content rather than calendar-style posts;
- one substantial article per month gives enough time for stronger research, examples, FAQs, internal linking, SEO/AEO/GEO structure and quality review.

## Publishing standard

Publish **one primary evergreen article per month**.

Do not use public slugs such as `week-28-*`, `september-blog-*`, or other calendar-dependent names. The month is only an internal planning cadence. Each live URL should target a durable parent question or problem.

Every monthly article should:

1. Answer one clear parent question or solve one identifiable child-learning problem.
2. Give a concise answer near the top for search, AEO and GEO.
3. Include age/stage context where relevant.
4. Include real examples rather than only definitions.
5. Give parents a practical home routine or action plan.
6. Explain common mistakes and when extra support may be needed.
7. Include useful FAQs.
8. Link naturally to the strongest relevant evergreen blog, parent guide, course page and assessment/demo path.
9. Avoid duplicating an existing canonical page.
10. Use the real publication date and a durable evergreen slug.

## Content mix target

Across a 12-month period, keep the blog balanced rather than publishing only phonics content:

- **Phonics / Reading / Spelling:** 4 articles
- **Grammar / Writing:** 2 articles
- **Communication Skills:** 2 articles
- **Public Speaking:** 1 article
- **English Classes / Parent decision support:** 1 article
- **Research / authority content:** at least 1 major cluster or article when there is a meaningful evidence-based topic

The exact mix can change based on Search Console performance, parent enquiries, new Tiny Steps programmes and emerging parent questions.

## Recommended monthly workflow

### Week 1 — Topic and intent

- confirm the article does not duplicate an existing canonical page;
- define one primary search intent and 3–5 supporting questions;
- identify internal links before drafting.

### Week 2 — Draft

- write the full parent-focused article;
- include examples, practical routines, FAQs and clear explanations;
- add research references where claims require evidence.

### Week 3 — SEO/AEO/GEO review

- title and meta description;
- quick-answer section;
- heading structure;
- FAQ quality;
- schema compatibility;
- internal links;
- canonical/indexing checks.

### Week 4 — Publish and monitor

- publish on the real date;
- regenerate sitemap/feed outputs if required by the repository workflow;
- verify the rendered article and metadata;
- monitor Search Console impressions and queries before selecting future refinements.
