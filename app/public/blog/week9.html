Week 9 Learning Spotlight

Blend, Punctuate & Paint with Words

Learners tuned their ears to consonant blends, used punctuation to guide expression, and described pictures using the five question words.

Phonics Focus

Beginning blends: bl, cl, st, tr, gr

Grammar Skill

Punctuation power: full stop, question mark, exclamation mark

Speaking Goal

Describe any picture using Who, What, Where, When, Why

Phonics

Blend the Beginning Sounds

We practised sliding two consonants together before adding the vowel. Students built words with blend cubes, played “blend hopscotch,” and read decodable sentences packed with bl-, cl-, st-, tr-, and gr- words.

  • Touch the two consonant letters together and say them as one sound (bl→ “bluh”).
  • Sort picture cards by their starting blend to reinforce listening skills.
  • Keep a running list of blend words you spot on signs, boxes, and menus.

Blend buddies this week: bl, cl, st, tr, gr. Ask your child to tap and read words like blend, clap, star, train, green.

Grammar

Punctuation Guides the Voice

Learners practised reading the same sentence three ways—ending with ., ?, or ! We matched punctuation marks to traffic-light style visuals and acted out how the voice changes.

  • While reading aloud, pause at the end of sentences and ask which mark fits best.
  • Make punctuation cards and hold them up as cues while your child reads.
  • Write a simple sentence and challenge your child to change the punctuation and meaning.

Full stop. Question mark? Exclamation! We’re practising how punctuation changes the voice and meaning.

Public Speaking

5W Picture Descriptions

We used busy scenes to answer Who? What? Where? When? Why? Learners organised their ideas into a short paragraph and practised expressive voices to keep listeners engaged.

  • Choose any family photo and ask the five questions together.
  • Encourage complete sentences that begin with the question word.
  • Record the description and play it back to celebrate clarity and detail.

Picture storytellers! Pick a photo and answer the 5Ws—Who, What, Where, When, Why—for a confident mini speech.

Support at Home in 15 Joyful Minutes

Revisit Week 9 skills with these playful routines. Choose one per day to keep learning strong.

Blend Relay

Write blends on index cards. Race to add vowel endings and read the words aloud.

  • Mix in nonsense words for an extra decoding challenge.
  • Keep score of how many blends you build in two minutes.
  • Switch roles so your child creates blends for you.

Punctuation Charades

Act out a sentence ending with ., ?, or ! and let your child guess the punctuation mark.

  • Use props or facial expressions to emphasise the feeling.
  • Invite siblings to join for a lively game.
  • Write the sentence afterwards and underline the punctuation.

5W Photo Journal

Print or draw a favourite photo, then jot down answers to the five questions under it.

  • Revisit the journal weekly to notice growing detail and vocabulary.
  • Share an entry with grandparents to practise public speaking.
  • End with a positive reflection: “Which detail made your story sparkle today?”

Progress You Can Celebrate

Look for smoother blending, expressive reading with the right punctuation, and picture descriptions filled with detail. Share the highlights with your Relationship Manager so we can cheer together.
