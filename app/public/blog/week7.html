Week 7 Learning Spotlight

Sounds that Glide & Feelings that Shine

From diphthongs that glide between vowel sounds to pronouns that keep sentences smooth and voices that express emotions, Week 7 was full of heart.

Phonics Focus

Diphthongs oi/oy and au/aw in words and phrases

Grammar Skill

Pronouns he, she, they, it replacing nouns in sentences

Speaking Goal

Express feelings clearly using voice, face, and body language

Phonics

Gliding Diphthongs

Learners sorted word cards by sound, matched oi/oy to noisy toys, and au/aw to cosy autumn scenes. We tapped out the vowel glides and read mini poems highlighting each pattern.

  • Use hand motions—point to your ear for oi/oy (noise) and give a warm hug for au/aw (saw).
  • Create a t-chart: words that end with oy vs. words with oi in the middle.
  • Spot the diphthongs during story time and celebrate each find with a cheer.

Diphthong detectives! Try reading: coin, soil, enjoy, boy, hawk, pause. Ask which sound glides the most.

Grammar

Pronouns in Action

We acted out short skits where pronouns replaced repeated nouns: “Riya has a kite. She flies it high.” Learners sorted pronoun cards and matched them to people, animals, and objects.

  • During conversations, pause and ask, “Which pronoun could replace that noun?”
  • Play a pronoun switch game: say a noun, and your child replies with the pronoun.
  • Create comic strips using speech bubbles that feature he, she, they, it.

Pronouns make sentences smooth! We practised he, she, they, it so stories flow without repeats.

Public Speaking

Share How You Feel

Learners used emotion cards to practise describing how they feel and why. We layered in facial expressions, body posture, and strong voice choices to communicate clearly.

  • Ask your child to describe a moment from the day and name the feeling plus the reason.
  • Mirror each other’s expressions to build empathy and awareness.
  • Record a short message for a family member sharing one proud feeling from the week.

Feelings first! Encourage your child to say “I feel… because…” with a confident voice and matching expression.

Support at Home in 15 Joyful Minutes

These quick ideas combine phonics practice with empathy building. Pick one each evening.

Sound Glide Sorting

Write diphthong words on slips. Sort them into bowls labelled oi/oy and au/aw.

  • Time the sort for a playful challenge.
  • Let your child invent one new word for each bowl.
  • Stick the lists near your reading corner for quick review.

Pronoun Patrol

During bedtime stories, take turns replacing repeated nouns with pronouns. Clap when the sentence still makes sense.

  • Make simple pronoun cards to hold up as you read.
  • Ask “Who or what does this pronoun stand for?”
  • Celebrate tricky switches like they/them.

Feelings Circle

At dinner, everyone shares one feeling and a matching sentence. Model calm breathing before speaking.

  • Keep a bowl of feeling words to pick from.
  • Encourage gestures that match the feeling.
  • End with a group high-five for brave sharing.

Progress You Can Celebrate

Celebrate when your child reads diphthong words smoothly, swaps pronouns naturally, and speaks openly about emotions. Share these milestones with your Relationship Manager so we can plan the next stretch goals together.
