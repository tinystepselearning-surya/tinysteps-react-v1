Week 3 Learning Spotlight

Blend, Build & Buddy Up

Learners stretched their decoding muscles, chose the right article for every noun, and practised friendly introductions. Keep the routines going at home with the ideas below.

Phonics Focus

Blend short-vowel CVC words with smooth finger slides

Grammar Skill

Use a, an, and the to match the noun

Speaking Goal

Introduce a classmate using name, likes, and a caring compliment

Phonics

Blend Every Sound Together

We practised tapping each sound and sliding it together for words like sun, map, and pet. Students built words with letter tiles, played “mystery bag” blending games, and raced to read CVC cards.

  • Touch each sound on your child’s fingers: /s/ /u/ /n/ then close the fist for the blended word.
  • Switch just one letter to make new words: cat → cot → cut.
  • Keep a mini stack of CVC cards near breakfast for quick rereads.

CVC superstars this week! Ask your reader to tap and blend these words: sun, map, pet, fog, rug. Celebrate each smooth slide.

Grammar

Choose the Right Article

Learners sorted pictures and matched them with a, an, or the. We listened for vowel sounds, compared general vs. specific nouns, and built sentences to show the difference.

  • Play “article match” during story time: pause and ask which article fits before you turn the page.
  • Sort household objects into two baskets labelled a and an.
  • Use the when you both know the item. Try: “Please pass the spoon we used for cereal.”

Article detectives! We hear the first sound to choose a, an, or the. Invite your child to explain the choice at dinner.

Public Speaking

Introduce a Friend with Heart

We practised interviewing a classmate, noting key details, and presenting them to the group. Eye contact, smiles, and kind adjectives were celebrated all week.

  • Role-play interviews at home. Swap who asks and who answers the questions.
  • Brainstorm warm phrases together: “This is my friend Riya. She loves drawing and shares her colours.”
  • Record a short video introduction to replay and notice confident body language.

We’re spotlighting friendship! Ask your child to introduce you today—name, favourite activity, and one kind compliment.

Support at Home in 15 Joyful Minutes

Pick one of these quick routines each day. They keep Week 3 learning playful while building your child’s confidence.

Blend & Build

Write CVC word families on sticky notes and slide them across the table as your child blends.

  • Swap the first or last letter to create a chain of new words.
  • Keep score of how many smooth blends you hear.
  • End with the silliest made-up word and laugh together.

Article Treasure Hunt

Label objects around the house with a, an, or the and have your child explain why.

  • Encourage them to listen for vowel sounds before sticking the label.
  • Invite siblings to check and cheer correct choices.
  • Switch roles and let your child quiz you next.

Family Spotlight

Choose one family member each night to introduce. Share their name, something they love, and why they’re special.

  • Use sentence stems like “This is…” or “They enjoy…”.
  • Add a friendly gesture—wave, handshake, or bow.
  • Celebrate brave voice and eye contact with applause.

Progress You Can Celebrate

Notice your child blending without stopping at each letter, choosing the right article in everyday talk, and speaking kindly about friends. Share these wins with your Relationship Manager so we can plan the next stretch goals together.
