Week 6 Learning Spotlight

Bossy R Readers & Picture-Perfect Speakers

This week we are blending phonics mastery with confident communication. Use the guidance below to keep the fun and learning flowing at home.

Phonics Focus

R-controlled vowels: ar, er, ir, or, ur

Grammar Skill

Prepositions that tell where: in, on, under, behind, next to

Speaking Goal

Picture talk stories using Who, What, Where, Colour, Feeling prompts

Phonics

Bossy R Changes the Sound

We helped learners hear how R guides the vowels in words like car, bird, and fork. Students sorted picture cards, read mini-stories, and built their own “Bossy R” word wall.

  • Blend each pattern slowly: say the vowel first, then add R, e.g., “a…r… ar”.
  • Play “Treasure Hunt R” by placing word cards around the room for children to collect and read aloud.
  • Celebrate when your child spots bossy R in books, cereal boxes, or road signs.

Bossy R is steering our words this week! ar (car), er (her), ir (bird), or (fork), ur (nurse). Ask your reader to hunt for each pattern around the house.

Grammar

Prepositions in Motion

Learners used toys and action stories to describe where objects were placed. The goal was to speak in full sentences while moving between positions.

  • Build a mini obstacle course and narrate each step: “The teddy is under the chair.”
  • Snap photos of a favourite toy in different spots and ask your child to label each one.
  • Encourage complete sentences. Swap roles so your child gives you the directions.

We are stretching spatial words: in, on, under, behind, next to. Try hiding a toy and asking for clues using prepositions!

Public Speaking

Picture Talk Storytelling

Children practised looking closely at a busy park scene, noticing details, and sharing short, clear sentences for each prompt.

  • Use the five prompts: Who do you see? What are they doing? Where are they? What colours pop out? How might they feel?
  • Invite your child to draw their own scene and present it to you or a grandparent.
  • Record a quick video reflection so your child can hear their confident voice.

Picture Talk power! We practise speaking in full, vivid sentences using the prompts: Who, What, Where, Colours, Feelings.

Support at Home in 15 Joyful Minutes

Choose one idea each day to reinforce what your child practised in class. Consistency—more than perfection—builds confident, curious communicators.

Mini Reading Routine

Pick a short story or decodable with bossy R words. Highlight the pattern in a bright colour, then reread together to build fluency.

  • Circle each R-controlled vowel pattern using sticky notes.
  • Ask, “Which bossy R sound was trickiest today?”
  • End with a silly sentence made from your favourite words.

Movement + Language Game

Create a path across the living room. Give verbal instructions with prepositions while your child acts it out—then swap!

  • Include actions such as “jump over the pillow” or “stand next to the plant.”
  • Use a timer for a playful challenge.
  • Invite siblings to take turns being the narrator.

Family Conversation Corner

Place an interesting photo on the fridge. During meals, ask one Picture Talk prompt and let everyone share a sentence.

  • Model expressive language—eye contact, clear voice, descriptive words.
  • Celebrate brave attempts and offer a sentence starter if your child gets stuck.
  • Capture favourite responses in a notebook to track growth.

Progress You Can Celebrate

Look for your child spotting bossy R words independently, using prepositions accurately, and sharing more detailed stories. Share these wins with your Relationship Manager so we can plan the next stretch goals together.
