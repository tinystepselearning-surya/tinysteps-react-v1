Week 10 Learning Spotlight

Chunk, Agree & Share a Story

Our final unit blended advanced decoding, grammar polish, and expressive speaking. Learners chunked multisyllable words, matched subjects with the right verbs, and presented a favourite book.

Phonics Focus

Chunk longer words into syllables to read smoothly

Grammar Skill

Subject–verb agreement with he/she/they and plural nouns

Speaking Goal

Show & Tell: My Favourite Book with summary and opinion

Phonics

Chunk Longer Words

We clapped, tapped, and boxed syllables in words like sunshine, picnic, and remember. Learners used flexible chunking—prefix, base, suffix—to build stamina while reading longer texts.

  • Mark syllable breaks with dots or scoops to help eyes track each chunk.
  • Practise with names of family members, favourite foods, or places.
  • Celebrate when your child self-corrects by re-chunking a tricky word.

Syllable power! Try chunking these together: picnic, robot, fantastic, remember, cupcake. Clap each part as you read.

Grammar

Match the Subject & Verb

Students built sentences with pronoun cards and verb cards, checking that singular subjects use singular verbs and plural subjects use plural verbs. We played “Agreement Detective” to spot and fix errors.

  • During conversation, model and repeat: “She likes… They like…”
  • Write sentences together and underline the subject and verb to ensure they agree.
  • Turn mistakes into a game—does the sentence need an S or does it stay the same?

Grammar focus: subject–verb agreement! She likes, he plays, they share. Listen for the endings at home.

Public Speaking

Favourite Book Spotlight

Learners prepared a Show & Tell about a beloved book. They shared the title, characters, favourite part, and why they recommend it, while practising eye contact and clear voices.

  • Create a simple outline: Title, Characters, Favourite Part, My Recommendation.
  • Practise holding the book as a prop and pointing to pictures while speaking.
  • Invite your child to end with a question for the audience to spark discussion.

Book lovers unite! Ask your child to introduce their favourite book—what happens, who’s in it, and why friends should read it too.

Support at Home in 15 Joyful Minutes

Cap off the term with these routines to keep reading and speaking progress steady through the break.

Syllable Snack Time

Use snack names to count and clap syllables before eating. Make it a friendly competition.

  • Try stretching multisyllable treats like “banana” or “paneer roll.”
  • Let your child invent new snacks and clap the syllables.
  • Chart the results to see which snack has the most beats.

Agreement Detective

Write sentences with intentional mistakes (“They runs fast.”). Ask your child to fix them.

  • Swap roles so your child writes a sentence for you to correct.
  • Underline the subject and verb once it’s correct.
  • Read the sentence aloud to hear how the change sounds.

Family Book Club

Gather a few favourite books. Each family member shares a quick recommendation using the class outline.

  • Record the presentations to share with relatives.
  • Keep a running list of “books we want to read next.”
  • Celebrate with a cosy reading session afterwards.

Progress You Can Celebrate

Notice your child chunking new words confidently, correcting subject–verb slips independently, and presenting favourite books with sparkle. Share these victories with your Relationship Manager so we can plan the next chapter.
