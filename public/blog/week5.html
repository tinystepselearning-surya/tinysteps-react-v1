Week 5 Learning Spotlight

Magic-E Makers & Story Weavers

Learners discovered how silent e changes vowel sounds, compared sentences in present and past tense, and shaped stories with clear beginnings, middles, and endings.

Phonics Focus

Magic-e turning tap → tape and kit → kite

Grammar Skill

Switch between simple present and simple past verbs

Speaking Goal

Tell a short story using the sequence: beginning, middle, end

Phonics

Meet the Magic-E Partner

We explored how silent e stretches vowel sounds, building words on flip cards (cap → cape, rid → ride). Learners read partner passages and highlighted every magic-e pattern they spotted.

  • Fold paper strips to reveal magic-e and listen for the sound change.
  • Sort words into two piles: short vowel vs. magic-e long vowel.
  • Challenge your child to find magic-e words in bedtime stories.

Magic-e made our vowels sing! Try reading these pairs together: cap/cape, kit/kite, hop/hope, mad/made.

Grammar

Present vs. Past Tense

Students acted out mini skits to show “I walk” versus “I walked.” We practised adding -ed, learned a few irregular verbs, and wrote twin sentences to compare tenses.

  • Ask, “What are we doing now? What did we do yesterday?” to practise both tenses.
  • Highlight the ending in past tense sentences so your child notices the change.
  • Make a two-column chart on the fridge to list present and past verbs.

Grammar time travel! We practised switching from “I jump” to “I jumped” and noticed how -ed shows the past.

Public Speaking

Storytelling with B-M-E

Learners mapped a story mountain, rehearsed telling what happened first, next, and finally, and practised clear voices for each part of their tale.

  • Use three picture cards to prompt beginning, middle, and end details.
  • Encourage expressive voices—whisper for suspense, brighten for the happy ending.
  • Invite your child to retell a favourite cartoon episode using B-M-E.

Story weavers at work! Ask for a three-part tale tonight: What happened first, next, and finally?

Support at Home in 15 Joyful Minutes

Consistent, low-pressure practice keeps Week 5 skills strong. Choose one routine to enjoy together.

Magic-E Makeover

Write short-vowel words on cards and let your child add e stickers to transform the sound.

  • Read each pair aloud and ask which word has the long vowel.
  • Draw a quick illustration to match the new word.
  • Set a timer for a playful “word makeover” challenge.

Tense Swap Journal

Keep a mini notebook. One page says “Today I…” (present), the next says “Yesterday I…” (past).

  • Write one sentence in each column after school.
  • Circle the verb ending to highlight the tense.
  • Share the journal with grandparents during calls.

Family Story Circle

Pass a storytelling baton (any object). Each person adds the next part of the story: beginning, middle, end.

  • Use a sand timer to keep each turn short and exciting.
  • Celebrate descriptive words and expressive voices.
  • Illustrate the story afterwards for a keepsake.

Progress You Can Celebrate

Notice your child reading magic-e words smoothly, switching between present and past tense with ease, and telling stories that flow from start to finish. Share highlights with your Relationship Manager so we can build on the momentum.
