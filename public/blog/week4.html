Week 4 Learning Spotlight

Digraph Detectives & Descriptive Voices

This week’s lessons helped learners hear two-letter teams, add descriptive sparkle to sentences, and share a favourite toy with confidence.

Phonics Focus

sh, ch, th, and wh digraphs in words and sentences

Grammar Skill

Adjectives that tell colour, size, texture, and feeling

Speaking Goal

Show & Tell stories about “My Toy” using clear structure

Phonics

Two Letters, One Sound

We sorted picture cards, played “mystery sound” with digraphs, and built words like ship, chin, math, and whale. Learners highlighted digraphs in mini stories to show they can spot sound teams in context.

  • Underline digraphs while reading and say the sound together.
  • Play “switch the digraph” by swapping sh/ch/th/wh at the start of a base word.
  • Use hand motions to reinforce each digraph (e.g., pretend to shush for sh).

Digraph detectives on duty! This week’s team words: ship, chin, path, whale, brush, when. Ask which digraph feels trickiest.

Grammar

Describe with Juicy Adjectives

Students built adjective word banks and paired them with nouns to create vivid sentences. We practised using colour, size, texture, and emotion words to paint mental pictures.

  • Play “Adjective Pass” by handing an object around and adding a describing word with each turn.
  • Sort adjectives into categories—colour, size, shape, feeling.
  • Encourage full sentences: “I have a sparkly, tiny car.”

Adjectives add magic! Try describing snacks tonight: crunchy apples, creamy yogurt, sweet mango.

Public Speaking

Show & Tell: My Toy

Learners outlined their talks with three prompts—What is it? Why do I love it? How do I play with it? We rehearsed expressive voices and steady pacing to keep listeners engaged.

  • Use a simple cue card with the three prompts to guide practice.
  • Record a rehearsal so your child can notice their smile and volume.
  • Invite an audience—family members, soft toys, or a video call with grandparents.

Show & Tell stars! Ask your child to share about a favourite toy using: What is it? Why do I love it? How do I play with it?

Support at Home in 15 Joyful Minutes

Short bursts of practice keep Week 4 skills fresh. Choose one idea that fits your family rhythm today.

Digraph Hunt

Stick the digraphs sh/ch/th/wh on the wall. Call out a word and let your child race to tap the matching team.

  • Add movement—hop, tiptoe, or dance to the correct card.
  • Let your child be the caller for the next round.
  • Keep a running list of new digraph words you spot together.

Adjective Chef

Describe tonight’s meal using texture and taste adjectives. Invite your child to add one new describing word.

  • Make a quick chart on paper with columns for each adjective type.
  • Encourage comparisons: “The soup is smoother than the rice.”
  • Draw the meal and label it with adjectives after dinner.

Toy Talk Stage

Set up a mini stage with a cloth or box. Give your child a microphone (spoon!) and let them rehearse their toy speech.

  • Clap or snap after each section to build confidence.
  • Prompt for eye contact by placing audience toys at different spots.
  • End with a family cheer for expressive speakers.

Progress You Can Celebrate

Look for your child spotting digraphs instantly, adding richer adjectives to conversations, and sharing about favourite items with a calm, clear voice. Share wins with your Relationship Manager to plan next steps together.
