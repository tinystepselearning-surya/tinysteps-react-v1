Week 2 Learning Spotlight

Short Vowels, Action Verbs & Full Sentences

Learners stretched their listening ears, moved their bodies with energetic verbs, and practised speaking in complete thoughts. Use these highlights to keep the momentum going at home.

Phonics Focus

Blend and read short vowel CVC words with smooth slides

Grammar Skill

Spot action words and match them to real-life movements

Speaking Goal

Build “who + action” sentences with clear voices

Phonics

Learn the Short Vowel Sounds

We revisited SATPIN letters and tuned our ears to the short vowel sounds a e i o u. Learners used counters to tap each sound, then blended words like cat, pen, pig, top, and sun.

  • Stretch the middle sound together: /c/ – /ă/ – /t/ → “cat”. Ask, “What vowel did you hear?”
  • Play “mystery vowel”: say two consonants and let your child choose a vowel card to build a real word.
  • Sort picture cards by vowel sound and cheer each confident blend.

Vowels are the heart of words! Ask your reader to tap and blend these CVC stars: cat, pen, pig, top, sun. Which vowel was the trickiest?

Grammar

Fun with Action Words

Action verbs took centre stage! Children matched pictures to verbs, acted them out charades-style, and built mini sentences to describe what they saw.

  • Play a guessing game: act out run, read, or draw and invite your child to name the verb.
  • Make a quick verb chart for morning routines: “I brush, I pack, I zip.”
  • Switch nouns to create new sentences: “The cat sleeps.” → “The dog sleeps.”

Verbs make sentences move! We are jumping, eating, playing, and reading our way through Week 2. What action word did your child love most?

Public Speaking

Say It in a Full Sentence

Learners practised pairing a “who” with a “doing” word to make complete ideas. We added gestures, friendly eye contact, and expressive voices for each new sentence.

  • Use picture prompts: “Who is in the picture? What are they doing?” Encourage a full answer.
  • Build a sentence tower — write “who”, “verb”, and “where” on sticky notes and stack them together.
  • Record a one-sentence update each evening. Replay it to notice strong, clear speech.

Big voices start with complete sentences. Try “who + action” today: “I am painting.” “She is skipping.” Share the proudest sentence with us!

Support at Home in 15 Joyful Minutes

Pick one of these mini routines each day to reinforce Week 2 learning. Short, playful sessions keep skills fresh and fun.

Vowel Sound Sort

Lay out vowel cards (a, e, i, o, u). Say a word and have your child place it under the correct vowel.

  • Use toy names or snack items to keep it relevant.
  • Swap roles and let your child test you next.
  • Celebrate each correct match with a high-five.

Action Word Freeze

Call out a verb and dance to it. When you say “freeze”, your child must shout a sentence using that verb.

  • Mix in quiet verbs like “read” or “think” for variety.
  • Invite siblings to be the caller for extra laughter.
  • End with a calm breathing verb: “breathe in, breathe out”.

Sentence Builders

Place “Who?”, “What doing?”, and “Where?” cards on the table. Pick one from each stack and speak the sentence aloud.

  • Write silly combinations together to encourage creativity.
  • Ask for a second sentence that adds more detail.
  • Record the best lines to share with family.

Progress You Can Celebrate

Notice when your child blends without guessing the vowel, names verbs in daily routines, and speaks in full sentences during play. Share these wins with your Relationship Manager so we can set the next stretch goals together.
