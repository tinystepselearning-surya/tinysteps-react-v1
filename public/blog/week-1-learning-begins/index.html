Week 1 – Learning Begins!

Your child’s Tiny Steps journey kicks off with powerful foundations across phonics, grammar, and public speaking. Here’s what we explored in Week 1.

Phonics Week 1 – Discover the First Sounds: S, A, T, P, I, N!

We start with the six foundation sounds — S, A, T, P, I, N. These letters help children build their first words and confidence in reading.

Classroom Snapshot

Visual focus: Each letter (S A T P I N) in pastel bubbles with a friendly icon — snake, apple, tap, pen, igloo, net.

Parent tip: Say each sound softly and stretch it – /s/ /a/ /t/ – then blend → sat!

At-home practice

Trace the letters with your child, say the sounds together, and blend to make words like sat, pin, nap, and tap.

Grammar Week 1 – Meet the Naming Words!

Children explore nouns – the names of people, places, animals, and things. Recognizing nouns builds sentence sense.

Classroom Snapshot

Four pastel cards help us sort: person – teacher, place – school, animal – dog, thing – ball.

At-home practice

Ask your child to look around the room and name five nouns they can see. Turn it into a treasure hunt!

Public Speaking Week 1 – How to Introduce Yourself Confidently

We begin with the simplest speech skill – introducing ourselves with eye contact, a smile, and a clear voice.

Classroom Snapshot

Children practise with pastel speech bubbles: “I am 6 years old.” “I love drawing.” “My favourite colour is blue.”

At-home practice

Encourage your child to stand tall, smile, and share their name, age, and one fun fact. Confidence begins with a warm “Hello!”.
